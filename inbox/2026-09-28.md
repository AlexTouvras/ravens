- **claim:** THL’s living rotavirus-vaccine instructions for professionals (RotaTeq in the national programme; page last updated 5 May 2026) say infants in the programme receive the vaccine free of charge at 6–31 weeks of age. THL recommends doses at 2, 3 and 5 months; if that is not possible, the first dose may be given from 6 weeks of age and must be given before 12.0 weeks, the third dose before 32.0 weeks, and the minimum interval between doses is four weeks. The vaccine is oral, never injected; if the infant regurgitates the dose, do not repeat it. Do not feed immediately before the dose (regurgitation risk); food, fluid, or breast milk do not reduce effectiveness. Defer if the infant has fever, diarrhoea, or vomiting. A previous intussusception, a congenital GI malformation that may predispose to intussusception, or immunosuppression are contraindications. Caregivers should seek a doctor for sudden colicky pain, vomiting, bloody/mucous stool, abdominal distension, or a drop in general condition (intussusception signs). This finding is the first-dose window / 2-3-5-month series rule, not a week-plan and not the already-filed RSV bronchiolitis same-day-care note.
