#### FIND-20261005-005

- **title:** ApproximateDistinctCount() now runs on Import and Direct Lake; exact distinct count stays the precision path
- **claim:** Chris Webb’s 4 Oct 2026 post says Import mode and Direct Lake mode semantic models now support the ApproximateDistinctCount() DAX function, which previously applied to DirectQuery sources. On a Direct Lake model with a 1.4 billion row fact table, his distinct count of Total Amount by Date took about 6 seconds, about 82 seconds of CPU, and 206,294 KB; the approximate distinct count took about 4 seconds and about 48 seconds of CPU, with higher memory (1,063,476 KB). He states the approximate values are only about 1.6% away from the actual values, and that stakeholders who require exact figures will reject that error. His workaround is a field parameter that defaults to the approximate count and lets the reader switch to an exact distinct count. This is the DAX availability note, not a Microsoft Learn function page (that URL 404’d this run).
- **why_it_matters:** Power BI portfolio — high-cardinality distinct counts on Import or Direct Lake boards can use ApproximateDistinctCount for speed, and measures that must stay exact (financial totals, low-cardinality categories) should remain DISTINCTCOUNT or offer an explicit switch
