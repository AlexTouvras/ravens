Daily symptoms must last more than two weeks to count as a disorder; 50–80% of mothers have transient tearfulness / sadness / anxiety in the first days (“herkistyminen” / baby blues) that usually fades within a couple of weeks. Contact neuvola or the health centre if symptoms last more than two weeks or are severe (suicidal thoughts, insomnia, hopelessness). Treatment is the same as for depression generally, plus support for parent–infant interaction; most antidepressants can be used while breastfeeding.
