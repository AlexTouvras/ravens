# Deload during lean cuts

## Guidance

- When bodyweight is trending down and sleep is short, cut weekly hard sets about 30% for 5–7 days before adding cardio.
- Keep protein and steps steady; treat the deload as training stress management, not a diet break unless hunger collapses.

## Rationale

Progressive overload fails when recovery debt compounds a calorie deficit. A planned volume cut preserves the lift pattern without the cost of random extra conditioning.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Not injured mid-cycle with pain that needs medical care — that is stop/modify, not a cosmetic deload.
- Not a bulk phase with good recovery — use normal progressive overload.
- Pregnancy programming uses separate safety rules; do not equate pregnancy fatigue with a lean deload.
