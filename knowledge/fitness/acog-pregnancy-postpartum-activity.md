# ACOG — ≥150 min/week moderate activity in pregnancy and postpartum

## Guidance

For household pregnancy / postpartum planning alongside mealplan (not a substitute for obstetric care):

- Target at least **150 minutes per week** of **moderate-intensity** aerobic activity during pregnancy and the postpartum period, **spread through the week**.
- People who were already doing **vigorous** activity before pregnancy may continue with obstetric oversight — do not invent a blanket ban or a blanket “keep maxing” rule.
- After delivery, **resume exercise gradually as soon as medically safe**, depending on delivery mode and complications; some can restart light activity within days when cleared by their clinician.
- **Pelvic-floor exercises** may start in the **immediate postpartum** period unless a clinician says otherwise.
- Do **not** apply non-pregnant hypertrophy strength defaults (e.g. ACSM ≥80% 1RM emphasis) as pregnancy/postpartum programming without clinical guidance.

## Rationale

ACOG Committee Opinion No. 804 restates durable public clinical guidance aligned with U.S. Physical Activity Guidelines: weekly moderate aerobic volume, continuity of prior vigorous activity with care, and staged postpartum return including early pelvic-floor work. That survives headline churn and is stable enough for mealplan household fitness planning.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Absolute or relative obstetric contraindications, complications, or clinician instructions to restrict activity — clinical advice wins.
- This is **not** medical advice or individualized rehab; it is a planning baseline from a professional committee opinion.
- Competitive athletes pushing beyond typical “vigorous” definitions need specialist oversight — this note does not set elite intensity caps.
- Non-pregnant `lean` hypertrophy templates stay on the ACSM volume note; cross-link rather than merge.
