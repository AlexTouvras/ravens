# ACSM 2026 — ~10 weekly sets per muscle for hypertrophy

## Guidance

- Train **all major muscle groups at least twice per week**; consistency beats complex periodization for general healthy adults.
- For **hypertrophy** goals (lean / high-protein templates): aim for about **~10 hard sets per muscle group per week**, distributed across sessions.
- For **strength** emphasis: prefer heavier loads (**≥80% 1RM**) for about **2–3 sets** per exercise.
- Bands, bodyweight, and home setups count — gym machines are optional for health/fitness outcomes.
- Individualize load and volume for adherence; do not chase failure/advanced techniques as mandatory for average healthy adults.

## Rationale

ACSM’s first major resistance-training Position Stand update since 2009 (announced 2026-03-17) synthesizes a large evidence review into simple weekly frequency and volume targets. Those targets are durable enough to anchor mealplan `lean` programming without copying influencer challenges.

## Limits / do not apply when

- **Pregnancy / postpartum** — use pregnancy-safe activity rules; do not apply ≥80% 1RM or aggressive hypertrophy volume without clinical guidance.
- Injury, pain, or medical restrictions — stop/modify with a qualified professional; this is not rehab programming.
- Sport-specific periodization for competitive athletes — the Position Stand’s “consistency first” message is for healthy adults’ general strength/hypertrophy/performance, not peak peaking cycles.
- Low-confidence social-media set/rep schemes that contradict the Position Stand — drop or signal only.
