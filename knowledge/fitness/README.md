# Fitness

Strength and recovery programming for lean goals, plus pregnancy-safe activity guidelines for household planning. Mealplan modes (`lean`, `pregnancy_nourish`) still apply. Existing pregnancy food-safety notes live under `food/` (off the daily watchlist).

## Current guidance

- ACSM 2026: major muscles ≥2×/week; ~10 weekly sets/muscle for hypertrophy; ≥80% 1RM when strength is the goal — [acsm-weekly-volume-hypertrophy](./acsm-weekly-volume-hypertrophy.md)
- ACOG: ≥150 min/week moderate aerobic activity in pregnancy and postpartum; gradual medically safe return; early pelvic-floor work — [acog-pregnancy-postpartum-activity](./acog-pregnancy-postpartum-activity.md)
- Finland postpartum week 3: walking plus pain-free pelvic-floor; brisker sport after the jälkitarkastus; C-section abs from 4–5 weeks — [fi-postpartum-walking-pelvic-floor](./fi-postpartum-walking-pelvic-floor.md)
- NSCA: same basic strength-and-conditioning principles apply regardless of sex; no menstrual-cycle-synced periodization needed — [nsca-no-menstrual-cycle-periodization](./nsca-no-menstrual-cycle-periodization.md)
