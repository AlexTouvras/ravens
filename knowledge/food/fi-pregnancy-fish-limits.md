# Finnish pregnancy fish limits are species-specific

## Guidance

For people who are pregnant, breastfeeding, or of childbearing age (Ruokavirasto cohort), mealplan fish templates should follow Finnish Food Authority limits — not a single “2×/week any fish” rule:

- **No special limit:** farmed fish; inland särke (roach).
- **Baltic wild:** at most **once per two months** for wild salmon (luonnonlohi), trout (taimen), lamprey (nahkiainen), or large herring (>19 cm ungutted).
- **Baltic other listed species** (e.g. kuha, lahna, siika, ahven, hauki, muikku): about **3–4 times per month**, varying species.
- **Inland listed species** (kuha, lahna, siika, ahven, hauki, muikku): **at most twice per week**, varying species; kuore about twice per month.
- Prefer variety across species; adult portion baseline in the guidance is ~100 g.

## Rationale

Ruokavirasto’s consumer fish page (updated 2025-01-02) sets PFAS/dioxin/mercury-aware exceptions on top of the general 2–3×/week fish advice. Pregnancy/pescatarian week plans for Asola/Vantaa need those species caps, not influencer “eat more salmon” heuristics.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Local water bodies with elevated mercury/PFAS advisories — follow municipal/ELYcenter restrictions over the national default.
- Non-pregnant household members without the fertile-age constraints — general population and under-school-age tables differ; do not copy pregnancy caps onto everyone blindly.
- Medical nutrition therapy under a clinician — this note is public-authority meal-planning guidance, not individualized clinical care.
- Outside Finland — use the local food-safety authority; do not export Ruokavirasto Baltic rules unchanged.
