# FI employers increasingly pair a cloud data warehouse with named Power BI ownership

## Guidance

- When writing CVs, capability narratives, or BI/analytics applications aimed at the Finnish market, foreground **both** a cloud data warehouse (Snowflake or Microsoft Fabric) **and** named Power BI ownership (semantic models, reports, real-time dashboards) — do not lead with Power BI alone or the warehouse alone.
- Where the posting also names data-governance tooling (e.g. Collibra, Ellie), add a governance bullet to the narrative rather than treating warehouse + BI as the whole story — UPM's Aug 2026 posting shows this is now an explicit third axis, not just warehouse + visualization.
- Treat this as market-language positioning guidance, not a capability framework — pair it with [uk-ddat-data-role-language](./uk-ddat-data-role-language.md) skill-ladder wording rather than replacing it.

## Rationale

Four named Finnish employers posted this pairing within roughly two weeks in August 2026: Stora Enso ("AI-assisted analytics" framing, posting expired unresolved), K-ryhmä (GenAI + Snowflake retail analytics), ABB (Snowflake SQL platform into Power BI semantic models, industrial), and UPM (Snowflake + DBT + Fabric + Power BI + governance tooling, industrial/forest products). Three of the four are independently corroborated named postings spanning retail and two industrial employers, which is enough repetition to treat "warehouse + Power BI" as a recurring FI market pattern worth CV language, not a one-off ad's wording.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Sample is four job postings over ~2 weeks in Helsinki/Tampere, Aug 2026 — small and time-boxed; re-check after 30 days that the pattern is still recurring before treating it as a lasting market norm.
- All four employers are Finnish; do not generalize to other Nordic or EU markets without fresh corroboration there.
- Do not treat any single posting (this note's own source findings included) as a standing capability framework — the individual job ads remain time-bound signals ([SIG-20260821-002](../../signals/SIG-20260821-002.md), [SIG-20260825-003](../../signals/SIG-20260825-003.md)) with their own apply-by deadlines.
- If postings stop repeating this pairing over the next month, mark this note `superseded` rather than leaving stale guidance in force.
