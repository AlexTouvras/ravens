# Mirror UK DDaT data-role capability language

## Guidance

- When writing CVs, capability narratives, or Delivery Lead / analytics applications aimed at UK or DDaT-influenced employers, pull skill and role-level wording from the live Government Digital and Data Profession Capability Framework — not from generic “data analyst” buzzword lists.
- Treat **data analyst**, **data engineer**, and **analytics engineer** (within the data role group, with Civil Service grades surfaced) as distinct ladders; map your evidence to the framework’s skills rather than collapsing them into one title.
- Re-check the framework after major public updates (the May 2026 refresh is the current baseline cited in FIND-20260811-007) before recycling old bullet language.

## Rationale

DDaT is a primary public reference for UK digital/data profession skills. Aligning careerops language to its analyst/engineer/analytics-engineer definitions improves keyword fit and progression clarity without inventing private employer taxonomies.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Finnish / Nordic private-sector postings that use local or vendor-specific taxonomies — mirror the posting first; DDaT is a secondary vocabulary there.
- Non-data delivery roles (pure Scrum Master / PM hype) — out of watchlist scope; do not force DDaT data skills onto unrelated applications.
- Framework pages that change skill definitions — update this note from a new FIND rather than assuming May 2026 wording forever.
