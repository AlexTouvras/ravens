# Finland — milk-only infants may stool several times a day or every 5–7 days; straining then a normal stool is not constipation

## Guidance

- Milk-only stool colour can run **yellow, brown, or even green-black**. Typical milk stool is **yellowish with “milk grains.”**
- Breastfed infants may pass stool **several times a day** or as seldom as every **5–7 days**.
- A baby who **strains, grunts, or even cries for 5–10 minutes** and then passes a **normal** stool is **not constipated** — the pelvic-floor muscles are still immature and the infant cannot yet relax them on cue. This eases with age **without treatment**.
- See a **doctor** for unintended **weight loss**, **blood or clots** in stool, stool that turns **black**, **fever** or lasting unusual tiredness, a **growth break**, repeated **vomiting** or blood in vomit, ongoing hard-to-live-with belly pain, or continuous watery diarrhoea.
- This is the **milk-only frequency / straining-is-not-constipation** rule, not a week-plan and not the already-filed spit-up / pyloric-stenosis note.

## Rationale

Duodecim Terveyskirjasto’s living “Lapsen normaali ulostaminen ja suolen toiminta” page (Lääkärikirja Duodecim, dated 1 Nov 2022) is standing Finnish public-health wording. The breast-milk 5–7 day gap, milk-grain colour range, and straining-then-normal-stool-is-not-constipation line hold if any single day’s headline is deleted.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: **milk-only infants** (`age-2-8w` now). Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage**. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
- **Finnish Terveyskirjasto / Duodecim** wording. Neuvola, the health centre, or 112 wins on the day.
- Not a diagnosis or a constipation-treatment plan. Do **not** apply the page’s **4–6 month complementary-food stool firming** (`age-2-6m`) or **toddler constipation / toddler diarrhoea** sections.
- Not the spit-up / pyloric-stenosis rule in [fi-infant-spit-up-pyloric-stenosis](./fi-infant-spit-up-pyloric-stenosis.md). Fever under 2 months still always needs a doctor — [fi-infant-crying-fever-never-shake](./fi-infant-crying-fever-never-shake.md).
- Do not treat this as a household week-plan.
