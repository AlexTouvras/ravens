# Finland — milia, baby acne, and cradle cap are usually harmless; navel warmth or blisters need care

## Guidance

- **Milia** (small white bumps, usually on the face) typically go in a **few weeks**. **“Newborn acne”** from maternal hormones appears at a **few weeks** of age in about **one in five** babies and fades over a **few months**. **Cradle cap** (taliköhnä, yellowish scalp scale) is harmless and almost always gone by **six months** — neuvola can give shampoo/oil how-to.
- Keep the **cord stump / navel dry**. Clean the fold with **water-moistened cotton**. The stump dries and falls in a few days, sometimes only after about **two weeks**. After it falls, a granulating wound can be cleaned or treated with **lapis at neuvola**.
- Seek care **the same day** if the skin around the navel becomes **red and warm**, or if a newborn / young infant develops **blisters, vesicles, spreading redness, or pus** (impetigo; herpes is possible at a few weeks).
- **Diaper rash** can start at a few weeks (more common later on solids): frequent changes, **water not soap**, pat dry, air baths, **thin zinc-oxide**. Show neuvola or a doctor if it **lasts over a week**, becomes **cratered**, or **spreads beyond the diaper area**.

## Rationale

Duodecim Terveyskirjasto’s living “Iho-ongelmat vauvoilla” page (Lääkärikirja Duodecim, dated 5 Jun 2024) is standing Finnish public-health wording. Harmless milia / baby acne / cradle cap, navel-warmth and blister/pus red flags, and the week-plus / cratered diaper-rash threshold hold if any single day’s headline is deleted. This is the skin / navel / diaper-rash rule, not a week-plan and not the first-days cord how-to.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: milia and navel care from the **first days**; baby acne and cradle cap from a **few weeks**; diaper-rash red flags from a **few weeks** (`age-2-8w` now). Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage**. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
- **Finnish Terveyskirjasto / Duodecim** wording. Neuvola, the maternity hospital, or 112 wins on the day.
- Not a diagnosis, an antibiotic plan, or a product haul. Routine first-days **stump cleaning** stays in [fi-newborn-first-days-care](./fi-newborn-first-days-care.md) (Terveyskylä dry cotton bud). This note is the later-weeks red-flag list.
