# Finland — file lapsilisä in OmaKela before birth; Kela learns of the birth from the register

## Guidance

- Child benefit (lapsilisä) is paid from the **beginning of the month after the child is born** until the end of the month the child turns **17**.
- You can apply in **OmaKela** for a child who is still expected. The decision is issued only **after the birth**, during the following month.
- Kela receives the birth from the **population information system** — the applicant does **not** need to notify Kela that the child has been born.
- Apply no later than **six months** after the period you want the benefit for.
- First-child rate on the living page: **EUR 94.88/month** plus a **EUR 26/month** under-3 supplement. File this alongside the birthing parent's vanhempainraha pre-birth claim in [fi-kela-vanhempainraha-before-birth](./fi-kela-vanhempainraha-before-birth.md).

## Rationale

Kela's living lapsilisä page is current Finnish-benefits protocol. The before-birth OmaKela filing, the register-notified birth (no separate birth notice), and the six-month deadline are standing timing rules — they hold if any single day's headline is deleted and they bite now in the late-pregnancy window.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: `late-pregnancy` (file now) through the **six-month** retroactive filing deadline after birth. Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage** — a human sets `Child born`, not this note.
- **Finnish Kela** benefit rules for people living permanently in Finland with the child. Other countries' child-benefit schemes and employer paid-leave policy are out of scope.
- Euro amounts (94.88 / 26) are as printed on the accessed page and change when Kela or Parliament updates the rates — do not treat them as a forever quote.
- Not medical guidance and not a substitute for OmaKela or Kela's own decision. Does not change [hus-call-before-labor](./hus-call-before-labor.md) go-in rules.
