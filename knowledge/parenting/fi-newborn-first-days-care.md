# Finland — first-days newborn care (back sleep, ≥8 feeds, cord, neck warmth)

## Guidance

- Change nappies as needed. Sleep the baby **on the back** (reduces SIDS risk; tummy time is for awake skin-to-skin). This restates [aap-safe-sleep-abcs](./aap-safe-sleep-abcs.md) in Finnish-hospital wording.
- Check and clean **skin folds** daily. Breastfeed at least **eight times** per 24 hours.
- Clean the **cord stump** primarily with a **dry cotton bud** at least once a day (more often if it discharges); it usually falls off within **two weeks**. A smelly stump is cleaned with a sting-free disinfectant twice a day.
- Check warmth at the **nape of the neck**, not by cool hands and feet. If the neck feels warm, the baby is warm enough.
- Do **not cut nails in the first two weeks** (infection risk at the nail fold). Use mittens if needed, but leave hands bare while feeding.
- Bathing at home is allowed even before the stump falls off, in about **37°C** water; dry the stump and folds afterwards.

## Rationale

Terveyskylä Naistalo's living newborn basic-care page (last reviewed 12 Jan 2026) is current Finnish first-days how-to. Feed frequency, cord care, the neck-temperature check, and the two-week nail rule are standing household items — they hold if any single day's headline is deleted and they fill the gap AAP safe-sleep and the discharge red-flag note do not cover.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: `age-0-72h` (adjacent prepare-now) through the first nights home and the **two-week** cord/nail window. Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage** — a human sets `Child born`, not this note.
- **Finnish hospital / Terveyskylä Naistalo** national guidance. The discharging ward's own printed instructions win on the day.
- Not a diagnosis or a substitute for the discharging ward or 112. Reduced feeding, jaundice, fever, or inconsolable crying still follow [fi-newborn-hospital-discharge](./fi-newborn-hospital-discharge.md) and [fi-newborn-jaundice-phototherapy](./fi-newborn-jaundice-phototherapy.md).
- Does not replace [aap-safe-sleep-abcs](./aap-safe-sleep-abcs.md) room-share / no-soft-bedding rules or the statutory neuvola home visit.
