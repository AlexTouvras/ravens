# Household week-plan — week 3 now, week 4 next

Muninn rewrites this note when Huginn's **Parenting week** block moves. Child born **2026-08-20**. Snapshot **2026-09-06**: day 17, week 3 (3–9 Sep). Coming week = week 4 (from 10 Sep). One-month mark 20 Sep. Seven-week tummy-time volume mark 8 Oct.

## Guidance

This week (week 3):

- Confirm the **LUVN 2–4 week nurse visit** (feeding, care, interaction, parent recovery) — [luvn-neuvola-2-4w-4-6w](./luvn-neuvola-2-4w-4-6w.md)
- Daily **vitamin D 10 µg** if not already started; lower the dose if formula ≥500 ml/day — [fi-infant-vitamin-d-from-2-weeks](./fi-infant-vitamin-d-from-2-weeks.md)
- Evening fussing is in the documented **2-week-to-1½-month rise** — cue-feed; fever under 2 months always needs a doctor; never shake — [fi-infant-crying-fever-never-shake](./fi-infant-crying-fever-never-shake.md)
- Short **supervised awake tummy time** (chest or lap first, then floor); quality over minutes — [fi-infant-tummy-time](./fi-infant-tummy-time.md)
- Expect **2–4 hour sleep stretches**, not a consolidated night — [fi-infant-sleep-2-4h-stretches](./fi-infant-sleep-2-4h-stretches.md)
- Development check: eye contact, moving arms and legs together — [thl-0-1-month-development-growth](./thl-0-1-month-development-growth.md)
- Growth: birth weight should be back by ~2 weeks; then about **150–200 g/week** (THL, not AAP ounces)
- Keep back sleep and ≥8 feeds; two-week nail rule is ending — [fi-newborn-first-days-care](./fi-newborn-first-days-care.md)
- OAE retest ~2 weeks if both ears failed — [fi-newborn-tsh-oae-screens](./fi-newborn-tsh-oae-screens.md)
- Names to DVV within three months — [fi-dvv-birth-hetu-automatic](./fi-dvv-birth-hetu-automatic.md)
- Other parent / spouse: vanhempainraha after birth if not filed — [fi-kela-vanhempainraha-before-birth](./fi-kela-vanhempainraha-before-birth.md)
- Postpartum return is **walking plus pain-free pelvic-floor**, not a lift program — [fi-postpartum-walking-pelvic-floor](../fitness/fi-postpartum-walking-pelvic-floor.md)

Coming week (week 4, from 10 Sep):

- Prep for **LUVN 4–6 week nurse+doctor** (growth, development, everyday care)
- Feeding still frequent (AAP 1-month visit: 8–12 breastfeeds/day) — [SIG-20260905-003](../../signals/SIG-20260905-003.md)
- Keep building **short tummy-time sessions** toward AAP 15–30 min/day by 7 weeks (8 Oct) — [SIG-20260906-001](../../signals/SIG-20260906-001.md)
- After a caesarean, abdominal-wall work may start at **4–5 weeks**; brisker sport still waits for the jälkitarkastus — [fi-postpartum-walking-pelvic-floor](../fitness/fi-postpartum-walking-pelvic-floor.md)
