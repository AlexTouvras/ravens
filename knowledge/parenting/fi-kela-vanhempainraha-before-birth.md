# Finland — birthing parent can file vanhempainraha in OmaKela before birth

## Guidance

- The **birthing parent** can apply for parental allowance (vanhempainraha) **already before the child is born**, at the same time as pregnancy allowance (raskausraha), or separately. Do not wait for hospital discharge to start the OmaKela filing.
- Each person claiming vanhempainraha must file **their own** application in OmaKela (or post form **SV 15**). The same OmaKela filing can also cover äitiysavustus and raskausraha.
- The **other parent**, the parent's spouse, or another guardian can apply **only after the child is born**.
- Apply no later than **two months** after each parental-leave period starts.

## Rationale

Kela's living vanhempainraha page (updated 4 Aug 2026) is current Finnish-benefits protocol. The before-birth filing window for the birthing parent, the after-birth rule for everyone else, and the two-month deadline are standing timing rules — they hold if any single day's headline is deleted and they bite now in the late-pregnancy window.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: `late-pregnancy` (file now) through each vanhempainvapaa period's **two-month** filing deadline after birth. Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage** — a human sets `Child born`, not this note.
- **Finnish Kela** benefit rules for people insured in Finland. Other countries' parental-leave schemes, employer paid-leave policy, and actual euro amounts are out of scope.
- Not medical guidance and not a substitute for OmaKela or Kela's own decision. Employer paid parental leave still needs the employer's own Kela claim.
