# Finland — 10 µg/day vitamin D from 2 weeks of age

## Guidance

- Give a **10 μg/day vitamin D supplement from 2 weeks of age**, year-round.
- **Reduce** the drop dose if the child also receives **500 ml or more** of infant formula / follow-on formula a day (see Ruokavirasto's separate infant dosing table: 500–800 ml → 6 μg; over 800 ml → 2 μg).
- Exclusive breast milk is otherwise enough until about 6 months **aside from vitamin D**.

## Rationale

Ruokavirasto's living infant-nutrition page (updated 14 May 2025) is current Finnish official guidance. The 2-week start and the formula-volume exception are standing household rules, not a news cycle.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: from **2 weeks** through infancy (`age-2-8w` and later). Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage**.
- **Finnish Ruokavirasto** dosing. Neuvola's own printed instruction wins if it differs on the day.
- Not medical advice for preterm, unwell, or clinician-restricted infants. Do not stack extra vitamin D on top of a product already dosed for the child's formula volume.
- Adult / pregnancy vitamin D stays under other notes; this is the child's drops.
