# Finland — rotavirus first dose from 6 weeks and before 12 weeks; series at 2, 3 and 5 months

## Guidance

- The national programme uses **oral RotaTeq**, free of charge for infants **6–31 weeks**. THL recommends doses at **2, 3 and 5 months**.
- If that schedule is not possible: the **first dose** may start from **6 weeks** and **must** be given **before 12.0 weeks**; the **third dose** before **32.0 weeks**; the **minimum interval** between doses is **four weeks**. No boosters after the primary series.
- It is **oral, never injected**. If the infant **regurgitates** the dose, **do not repeat** it.
- Do **not feed immediately before** the dose (regurgitation risk). Food, fluid, or breast milk **do not** reduce effectiveness.
- **Defer** the appointment if the infant has **fever, diarrhoea, or vomiting**.
- Seek a **doctor** for sudden colicky pain, vomiting, bloody or mucous stool, abdominal distension, or a drop in general condition (intussusception signs).
- This is the **first-dose window / 2-3-5-month series** rule, not a week-plan and not the already-filed RSV bronchiolitis same-day-care note.

## Rationale

THL’s living rotavirus-vaccine instructions for professionals (page last updated 5 May 2026) are standing Finnish public-health wording. The 6-week open / 12-week first-dose close, the 2/3/5-month series, “oral not injection,” and “do not repeat a regurgitated dose” hold if any single day’s headline is deleted.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: programme ages **6–31 weeks**; first dose **6 weeks–before 12.0 weeks**; third dose **before 32.0 weeks** (child tag `age-2-8w` now). Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage**. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
- **Finnish THL / neuvola** schedule. The clinic’s appointment and the vaccinating clinician win on the day. This is not a home-administration protocol.
- Not a diagnosis, an RSV note, or a reason to skip [under-2-month fever](./fi-infant-crying-fever-never-shake.md).
- Do **not** treat previous intussusception, a congenital GI malformation that may predispose to it, or immunosuppression as DIY contraindications to override — those are clinician gates. Do not apply preterm 25-week / 28-week breathing-monitor rules, maternal biologic-drug delays, or Kawasaki discussion as household schedule.
- Not a household week-plan.
