# Finland — statutory neuvola home visit within 1–14 days of birth for first-time parents

## Guidance

- Expect a **public-health-nurse home visit** from the wellbeing services county within **1–14 days** of the birth of a first child (1–7 days in urgent cases) — this is a statutory service under Government Decree 93/2026, not something to arrange privately.
- Add it to the arrival checklist alongside the existing safe-sleep setup: no booking action needed on the parents' side, but be ready to receive the visit at home during the first two weeks.
- The same decree also guarantees **15 scheduled health check-ups** for children under school age — a broader baseline worth knowing, even though only the first home visit is in the current age window.

## Rationale

Valtioneuvoston asetus 93/2026 (in force since 1 Feb 2026) is the current, official Finnish legal text governing neuvola (maternity and child health clinic) services. A statutory requirement in a living legal instrument is durable — it does not depend on any single week's news cycle and still holds unless the decree is amended or repealed.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Applies to **first-time families** specifically for the mandated home visit; the note does not cover subsequent-child visit timing, which the decree may treat differently.
- Age window: relevant while preparing during late pregnancy and through the **first 14 days** after birth (`age-0-72h`, extending slightly beyond that window to day 14). Re-check `knowledge/parenting/README.md` **Current stage** rather than assuming this note updates it — a human sets `Child born`, not this note.
- This is an administrative/legal fact, not medical guidance — pair with [aap-safe-sleep-abcs](./aap-safe-sleep-abcs.md) and neuvola's own clinical instructions for care specifics.
- If a later decree amendment changes the 1–14 day window, supersede this note rather than editing the date in place.
