# Finland — infant oral thrush is usually harmless; treat with local antifungal drops if needed and boil recurring dummies

## Guidance

- The most common mouth inflammation in children is candidal **thrush (sammas)**, especially in **infants**: pale coatings on the **tongue and inner cheeks**. It occurs in about **5%** of children and is itself a **harmless** infection.
- Treat locally with **antifungal drops when needed**. Mild cases follow **neuvola** advice.
- Recurring episodes often come from yeast on **dummies and mouthed toys** — **boil or replace** those sources.
- This is the **infant-thrush identification / dummy-boil / drop-treatment** rule, not a week-plan and not the already-filed dummy-wait-until-weight-rises milk-sufficiency note.

## Rationale

Duodecim Terveyskirjasto’s living “Suutulehdus lapsella” page (Lääkärikirja Duodecim, dated 27 May 2024) is standing Finnish public-health wording. Pale infant coatings as usually-harmless thrush, local drops when needed, and boil-or-swap recurring dummies hold if any single day’s headline is deleted.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: **small infants** when thrush typically shows (`age-2-8w` now). Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage**. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
- **Finnish Terveyskirjasto / Duodecim** wording. Neuvola, the health centre, or 112 wins on the day.
- Not a diagnosis or a product haul. Do **not** apply the page’s **herpes / enterovirus / aphthous** sections (older-child or febrile-blister rules) or its mild-case acidic-fruit / juice tip to a young infant.
- Not the dummy-**wait** until latch and weight rise in [fi-infant-milk-supply-dummy](./fi-infant-milk-supply-dummy.md). If burning-nipple mastitis may be Candida, also treat the parent — [fi-postpartum-mastitis-empty-12h](./fi-postpartum-mastitis-empty-12h.md).
