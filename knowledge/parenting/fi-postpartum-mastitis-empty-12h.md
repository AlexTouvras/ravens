# Finland — if mastitis has no fever, empty the breast; high fever or no ease in 12 hours needs a doctor

## Guidance

- Postpartum mastitis (mastiitti) occurs in about **3–6%** of breastfeeding mothers, usually after a **blocked duct** or **nipple cracks**, most often staphylococcal. A blocked duct starts as a tender, **sharply bordered red patch**; as it worsens, fever can rise to about **40 °C** and the whole breast becomes red and sore.
- If there is **no fever**, emptying the breast is enough: **feed first from the sore side** and pump if needed. A **cool pack** between feeds eases swelling.
- If **fever is high** or symptoms **do not ease within 12 hours** of frequent emptying, contact a **doctor or health centre** the same day to assess antibiotics (can be prescribed by phone when symptoms are short).
- The baby may **keep feeding** from the inflamed breast, including during antibiotics; the milk remains suitable.
- Afebrile **burning-nipple** mastitis can be Candida — then also **check the baby’s mouth** and treat both (see [fi-infant-oral-thrush-dummy](./fi-infant-oral-thrush-dummy.md)).
- An **abscess** (ongoing fever plus a separate tender lump despite antibiotics) needs drainage — that is a doctor visit, not home care.
- This is the **emptying / 12-hour / fever** rule, not a week-plan and not the already-filed milk-sufficiency note.

## Rationale

Duodecim Terveyskirjasto’s living “Rintatulehdus” page (Lääkärikirja Duodecim, dated 31 Mar 2026) is standing Finnish public-health wording. The afebrile empty-first line, the 12-hour / high-fever care-seeking threshold, and keep-feeding through treatment hold if any single day’s headline is deleted. The first-days lochia note only flags “red, hot breast plus high fever”; this note is the breastfeeding-window home-care vs doctor split.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: **breastfeeding parent** in the infant-feeding weeks (child tag `age-2-8w` now). Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage**. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
- **Finnish Terveyskirjasto / Duodecim** wording. Neuvola, the health centre, the maternity hospital, or 112 wins on the day.
- Not a diagnosis, an antibiotic plan, or a drug list. The source names typical 10–14 day agents — **do not apply those as household guidance**.
- Do **not** apply the page’s **periductal mastitis** (typically 40–50-year-old smokers, not breastfeeding) or mammography-after-treatment work-up.
- Not the lochia / endometritis fever split in [fi-postpartum-lochia-fever-red-flags](./fi-postpartum-lochia-fever-red-flags.md) and not the milk-sufficiency / dummy-wait rule in [fi-infant-milk-supply-dummy](./fi-infant-milk-supply-dummy.md).
- Do not treat this as a household week-plan.
