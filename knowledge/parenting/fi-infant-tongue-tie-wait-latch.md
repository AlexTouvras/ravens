# Finland — a tight-looking infant tongue-tie is not a preventive cut; give latch practice time; a lip-tie does not change breastfeeding

## Guidance

- The lingual frenulum is a **normal floor-of-mouth fold**. Appearance varies widely (published prevalence **0–12%**; **no shared diagnostic criteria**). A newborn frenulum often looks tight **without affecting eating or life** — then **no procedure**.
- Surgery assessment is only when an unusually tight frenulum **clearly limits tongue movement** *and* there is a **latch problem or breastfeeding pain**. Give **time and support for latch practice first**.
- Do **not** cut a tongue-tie or lip-tie in infancy to prevent later eating, sleep, crying, spit-up, speech, or bite problems — studies have **not** shown that benefit.
- **“Posterior tongue-tie”** is poorly defined and **must not** be a surgical indication.
- An infant **upper-lip frenulum** is a normal structure, **does not affect breastfeeding mechanics**, and does **not** need cutting to improve latch.
- If a cut is done, the infant can **feed immediately**. Do **not massage** the mouth scar (oral-aversion risk).
- This is the **tongue-tie / lip-tie / wait-on-practice** rule, not a week-plan and not the already-filed milk-sufficiency / dummy-wait note.

## Rationale

Duodecim Terveyskirjasto’s living “Vauvan kireä kielijänne ja huulijänne” page (Lääkärikirja Duodecim, dated 3 Mar 2025) is standing Finnish public-health wording. The wait-on-latch-practice line, no-preventive-cut rule, and lip-tie-does-not-change-mechanics line hold if any single day’s headline is deleted.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: **small infants** when a tight-looking frenulum is often raised (`age-2-8w` now). Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage**. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
- **Finnish Terveyskirjasto / Duodecim** wording. Neuvola, the paediatrician, ENT, or 112 wins on the day.
- Not a diagnosis or a reason to refuse a procedure when tongue movement is clearly limited *and* latch or pain is still a problem after practice. The page’s **older-child speech** (r / l / t / d) and **school-age lip-tie / orthodontics** sections are not this note.
- Not the delivery-room first-latch crawl / two-hour hand-express rule in [fi-first-latch-hand-express](./fi-first-latch-hand-express.md) and not the dummy-wait milk-sufficiency rule in [fi-infant-milk-supply-dummy](./fi-infant-milk-supply-dummy.md).
- Do not treat this as a household week-plan.
