# Finland — jälkitarkastus is at 5–12 weeks; Kela no longer needs a certificate

## Guidance

- Book the **jälkitarkastus** (postnatal check) for **everyone who gave birth**, at **5–12 weeks** after birth. A doctor or a trained nurse/midwife does it.
- The visit covers **recovery**, **birth injuries**, **mood / depression screening**, how **feeding and infant care** have started, and **contraception**.
- A **gynecological exam** can be offered but is **not required** after an uncomplicated birth and **can be declined**.
- A **jälkitarkastus certificate is no longer issued**. Kela no longer requires it for **vanhempainraha** after the family-leave reform — do not wait on paper for the benefit.
- This is the **parent’s postnatal-check window and Kela-certificate rule**, not the child’s LUVN 4–6 week clinic visit and not the walking / pelvic-floor sport-wait already on file.

## Rationale

Duodecim Terveyskirjasto’s living “Synnytyksen jälkitarkastus” page (Lääkärikirja Duodecim, dated 29 Feb 2024) is standing Finnish public-health wording. The 5–12 week window, who may do the visit, optional gyne exam, and the dropped Kela certificate hold if any single day’s headline is deleted. Not a week-plan.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: **5–12 weeks after birth** (parent postnatal check; child tag `age-2-8w` now). Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage**. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
- **Finnish Terveyskirjasto / Duodecim** wording. Neuvola, the maternity hospital, or 112 wins on the day.
- Not the LUVN **child** timetable in [luvn-neuvola-2-4w-4-6w](./luvn-neuvola-2-4w-4-6w.md) and not sport clearance — that staging stays in [fi-postpartum-walking-pelvic-floor](../fitness/fi-postpartum-walking-pelvic-floor.md).
- Not a diagnosis, a contraception prescription, or a substitute for OmaKela. Vanhempainraha filing timing is [fi-kela-vanhempainraha-before-birth](./fi-kela-vanhempainraha-before-birth.md).
- Do not treat this as a household week-plan.
