# Finland — baby blues should ease in about two weeks; go to neuvola if low mood is still daily or is severe

## Guidance

- **Postpartum depression** is a depressive episode that **begins within six weeks of birth** (about **10–20%** of mothers; **8–10%** of fathers). Daily symptoms must last **more than two weeks** to count as a disorder.
- **50–80%** of mothers have transient tearfulness / sadness / anxiety in the first days (“**herkistyminen**” / baby blues) that usually fades within **a couple of weeks**.
- Contact **neuvola or the health centre** if symptoms last **more than two weeks** or are **severe** (suicidal thoughts, insomnia, hopelessness). **Do not wait** for the 5–12 week jälkitarkastus.
- This is the **baby-blues vs PPD timing / neuvola** rule, not a week-plan and not the already-filed jälkitarkastus visit window.

## Rationale

Duodecim Terveyskirjasto’s living “Synnytyksen jälkeinen masennus” page (Lääkärikirja Duodecim, dated 5 May 2026) is standing Finnish public-health wording. The six-week onset window, the two-week baby-blues fade vs disorder line, and the neuvola / health-centre care-seeking rule hold if any single day’s headline is deleted.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: parent mood watch from the **first days through six weeks** after birth (child tag `age-2-8w` now). Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage**. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
- **Finnish Terveyskirjasto / Duodecim** wording. Neuvola, the health centre, the maternity hospital, or 112 wins on the day.
- Not a diagnosis, a screening-score cut-off, or a treatment / antidepressant / breastfeeding-drug plan. The source discusses therapy and medicines — **do not apply those as household guidance**.
- Not the parent’s **5–12 week** check window in [fi-jalkitarkastus-5-12w-no-kela-cert](./fi-jalkitarkastus-5-12w-no-kela-cert.md) and not the lochia / fever red flags in [fi-postpartum-lochia-fever-red-flags](./fi-postpartum-lochia-fever-red-flags.md).
- Do not treat this as a household week-plan.
