# Finland — waters breaking: call the maternity hospital first; pad-check at home

## Guidance

- Membranes rupture before contractions in **10–12%** of pregnancies. **60–80%** then start labour spontaneously within **1–2 days**; if they do not, labour is usually induced **24 hours** after rupture.
- If you suspect waters (trickle or suddenly watery discharge), **phone the maternity-hospital emergency line first** — do not drive in unannounced.
- At home you can place a **sanitary pad** and watch it soak.
- You are usually called in for a check at **12 hours** (CTG plus a GBS swab). If GBS is positive: IV antibiotic prophylaxis, and induction may start before 24 hours.
- If GBS is negative, the fetus is well, and there are no contractions, you can often still go home. Always phone before travelling in.
- HUS-catchment households still use **09 471 71500** as the call-first number in [hus-call-before-labor](./hus-call-before-labor.md). This note is the national waters protocol, not a replacement for that line.

## Rationale

Terveyskylä Naistalo's living waters-breaking page is current Finnish-hospital protocol for a household still in the late-pregnancy / labor window. Call-first, the pad check, the 12-hour review, and the usual 24-hour induction are standing checklist items — they hold if any single day's headline is deleted and they fill the gap the HUS contraction go-in note does not cover (waters can break with no regular contractions).

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: `late-pregnancy` / `labor` only. Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage** — a human sets `Child born`, not this note.
- **Finnish hospital / Terveyskylä Naistalo** national guidance. The delivering hospital's own phone number and printed instructions win on the day.
- Not a diagnosis or a substitute for the midwife on the line (or 112). Heavy bleeding, continuous severe pain, reduced fetal movement, or an urge to push still follow [hus-call-before-labor](./hus-call-before-labor.md) / 112.
- Does not set hospital routing (Naistenklinikka / Jorvi / Hyvinkää) or invent a birth date.
