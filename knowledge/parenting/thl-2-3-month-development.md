# THL — at 2–3 months the first intentional smile, head support when lying, and a two-handed grasp

## Guidance

- **2–3 months** (THL *Meille tulee vauva* table, via Terveyskirjasto): first intentional smile, cooing (ää, gyy), wants to socialize; supports the head when lying down and kicks both legs together; grasps objects with both hands and studies the hands; turns the head toward sound and follows a moving object with the eyes.
- The table says **deviations are common**. Development is individual. Do not use influencer “wonder week” lists.
- This is the next row after the **0–1 month** markers (involuntary smiles, eye contact, limbs moving together). It is not a week-plan.

## Rationale

The Terveyskirjasto living page is based on THL *Meille tulee vauva* (2023). The 2–3 month row is the same durable public-health table as the 0–1 month note, with an explicit “deviations are common.”

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: **2–3 months** (`age-2-6m`). While the baby is still in the **0–1 month** row, use [thl-0-1-month-development-growth](./thl-0-1-month-development-growth.md). Do not pull the 4–6 month row (laughing aloud, rolling, one-handed grasp) forward.
- **Corrected age** for preterm babies (from the due date), as the same page states.
- The same page says a baby this age mouths what they grasp. Keep choking, toxic, and dirty objects out of reach. That is a household limit, not a diagnosis.
- Not a milestone test. A missing smile, weak head support, or no turn toward sound still goes to neuvola — do not wait out a worry because the table says deviations are common.
- Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage**. Do not compute week of life from this note.
