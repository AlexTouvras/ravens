# Finland — established milk supply does not stop suddenly; wait on a dummy until weight is rising

## Guidance

- Breast-milk is usually enough when the baby latches well and is fed often. Once lactation is **established it does not stop suddenly** if feeding continues.
- Volume can dip with strong stress or maternal hormones. A healthy baby responds by wanting to **feed more often**; supply usually **returns within one or two days**. More frequent feeds are the expected repair — not an automatic formula top-up.
- Check intake first by **regular weighing**. Birth weight is typically regained within about **ten days** (see also [thl-0-1-month-development-growth](./thl-0-1-month-development-growth.md) ~2-week wording).
- At home, from the **fourth day** onward: at least **4–6 unmistakably wet nappies** per day.
- Contact **neuvola** if worried about intake or weight.
- Do **not offer a dummy** until breastfeeding is going and weight is rising. Constant dummy use can cut breast stimulation and slow gain. A dummy is for fussing that is not hunger — not a substitute feed.

## Rationale

Terveyskirjasto’s living “Vauvan ravitsemus” page is based on THL *Meille tulee vauva* (2023). The established-supply line, the 1–2 day recovery via more frequent feeds, the wet-nappy / weigh-in checks, and the dummy-wait rule are standing public-health items — they hold if any single day’s headline is deleted and they fill the cluster-feeding gap first-latch and first-days feed-count notes do not cover.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: **established lactation through the cluster-feeding / dummy-wait window** (`age-2-8w` now). This note is **not** the page’s 4–6 month complementary-food section (`age-2-6m`). Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage**.
- **Finnish Terveyskirjasto / THL** wording. Neuvola or 112 wins on the day.
- Not a diagnosis, a reason to refuse medically indicated formula, or a sleep-training method. Poor feeding, no weight regain, or a baby who seems ill still goes to neuvola.
- Does not replace [fi-first-latch-hand-express](./fi-first-latch-hand-express.md) (delivery-room first hours) or [fi-newborn-first-days-care](./fi-newborn-first-days-care.md) ≥8 feeds / 24h.
- Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
