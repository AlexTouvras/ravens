# Finland — first-time labor is usually 2h of 5-minute contractions; subsequent 1h of 10-minute contractions

## Guidance

- Labor starts with **contractions** or **waters breaking**. First-time and subsequent births use different “underway” thresholds.
- A **first-time** birth is generally considered underway when contractions have come for at least **two hours**, every **five minutes**, each lasting **one minute**.
- A **subsequent** birth is generally considered underway when contractions have come for at least **one hour**, every **ten minutes**, each lasting **one minute**.
- Labor can start slowly. Contractions may ease and then resume — it is safe to stay home and wait. If unsure whether to travel in, **phone the maternity hospital**.
- Call immediately for heavy bleeding, constant severe pain, suspected waters, painful regular contractions every **5–10 minutes**, reduced fetal movement, or sudden symptoms such as severe headache, visual disturbance, upper-abdominal pain, or intense itching of soles, palms, or abdomen.
- HUS-catchment households still use **09 471 71500** and the go-in-under-10-minutes rule in [hus-call-before-labor](./hus-call-before-labor.md). This note is the national first-time vs subsequent threshold, not a replacement for that line. Waters without regular contractions still follow [fi-waters-breaking-call-first](./fi-waters-breaking-call-first.md).

## Rationale

Terveyskylä Naistalo's living spontaneous-labor-start page is current Finnish-hospital protocol for a household still in the late-pregnancy / labor window. The first-time vs subsequent contraction windows and the phone-first rule when unsure are standing checklist items — they hold if any single day's headline is deleted and they fill the gap the HUS call-first / under-10-minutes note does not cover (national thresholds differ by parity).

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: `late-pregnancy` / `labor` only. Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage** — a human sets `Child born`, not this note.
- **Finnish hospital / Terveyskylä Naistalo** national guidance. The delivering hospital's own phone number and printed instructions win on the day.
- Not a diagnosis or a substitute for the midwife on the line (or 112). Heavy bleeding, continuous severe pain, reduced fetal movement, or an urge to push still follow [hus-call-before-labor](./hus-call-before-labor.md) / 112.
- Does not set hospital routing (Naistenklinikka / Jorvi / Hyvinkää) or invent a birth date. Subsequent-birth thresholds do not apply to a first child.
