# Finland — first latch: let the baby crawl to the breast; hand-express if no latch in two hours

## Guidance

- Newborns usually rest, then find the breast themselves. The best latch is when the baby is allowed to **crawl onto the breast at their own pace**, typically lying **prone on the parent's chest**.
- If the baby has **not latched within about two hours** of birth: **hand-express** the first drops so milk production gets a start and the baby does not tire; keep skin-to-skin; wait for hunger cues. Hospital staff will help.
- The same hand-express step applies if the baby goes to the paediatric ward, or stays in skin-to-skin but does not root — so the baby still gets **colostrum drops**.
- After the first latch, first-days feed frequency is still **≥8 breastfeeds / 24h** in [fi-newborn-first-days-care](./fi-newborn-first-days-care.md).

## Rationale

Terveyskylä Naistalo's living first-breastfeed page (last reviewed 9 Dec 2024) is current Finnish delivery-room protocol. The crawl-to-breast default, the two-hour latch window, and the hand-express-colostrum fallback are standing items — they hold if any single day's headline is deleted and they fill the gap first-days home care does not cover.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: `labor` (delivery-room first hours) and adjacent `age-0-72h`. Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage** — a human sets `Child born`, not this note.
- **Finnish hospital / Terveyskylä Naistalo** national guidance. The ward's own lactation support wins on the day.
- Not a diagnosis or a substitute for hospital staff. A baby who will not feed, is listless, or is jaundiced still follows [fi-newborn-hospital-discharge](./fi-newborn-hospital-discharge.md) and [fi-newborn-jaundice-phototherapy](./fi-newborn-jaundice-phototherapy.md).
- Does not replace [fi-newborn-first-days-care](./fi-newborn-first-days-care.md) home how-to (cord, neck warmth, two-week nails) or [aap-safe-sleep-abcs](./aap-safe-sleep-abcs.md) once the baby is sleeping on their back.
