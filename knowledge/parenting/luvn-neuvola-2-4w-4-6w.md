# LUVN — 2–4 week nurse visit, then 4–6 week nurse+doctor

## Guidance

- Espoo child-health clinic is **Länsi-Uudenmaan hyvinvointialue (LUVN)**, not HUS. HUS was the delivering hospital.
- Expect a **health-nurse** visit at **2–4 weeks of age**: care, feeding, parent–baby interaction, parenthood, and the birthing parent's recovery.
- Next is a **nurse and doctor** visit at **4–6 weeks**. That first clinic doctor exam complements the hospital discharge exam and follows growth, development, and everyday baby-care questions.
- Book / confirm with own neuvola (Lunna). Local slotting can differ; this page is the area timetable, not a personal appointment.

## Rationale

LUVN's living neuvola-visit page is the household's wellbeing-area timetable. The 2–4 week then 4–6 week sequence survives headline churn and is the calendar spine for week-of-life planning in Espoo.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: `age-2-8w` (about 2–8 weeks of life). Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage**.
- **LUVN only.** Other wellbeing areas use different weeks. The statutory first home visit (1–14 days) is a separate legal note: [fi-neuvola-home-visit-first-time-parents](./fi-neuvola-home-visit-first-time-parents.md).
- Not a diagnosis. The discharging ward, neuvola, or 112 still win on the day.
- Not HUS labour / Jorvi call-first guidance.
