# Finland — five-in-one (DTaP-IPV-Hib) at 3, 5 and 12 months

## Guidance

- THL recommends the **five-in-one** (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis, polio, Hib) at **3, 5 and 12 months**. The programme product is **Infanrix-Polio-Hib**. The dose is given as an injection. It is not the oral rotavirus series.
- If that timing is not possible: leave **2 months** between the first and second dose and **4 months** between the second and third.
- Bring the series forward only when it **must** move up (THL’s example is travel). Then it may start at **6 weeks**, with **at least 8 weeks** between the first and second dose, and the **third dose still at 12 months**.
- **Defer** the visit for fever or a febrile infection. Do not stop the series on your own. A suspected reason to skip a later dose goes to the wellbeing-services physician responsible for vaccinations.
- This is the **3 / 5 / 12 month injectable schedule**, not a week-plan and not the rotavirus note.

## Rationale

THL’s living five-in-one instructions for professionals (page updated 5 May 2026) are standing Finnish public-health wording. The 3, 5 and 12 month ages, the fallback gaps, and the 6-week accelerated start hold if any single day’s headline is deleted. The 2-month visit already on file is rotavirus, not an automatic extra five-in-one injection.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: usual doses at **3, 5 and 12 months**. Accelerated start from **6 weeks** only when the series must move up. The product may be used up to **age 5** in the programme; this note is the infant primary series (source tag `age-2-6m`). Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage**. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
- **Finnish THL / neuvola** schedule. The clinic’s appointment and the vaccinating clinician win on the day. This is not a home-injection protocol. Do not mix the syringe and the powder ampoule at home, and do not give a missed Hib component yourself.
- Not the oral rotavirus series at **2, 3 and 5 months** — see [fi-infant-rotavirus-2-3-5m](./fi-infant-rotavirus-2-3-5m.md). Not an RSV vaccine or antibody schedule.
- Do not apply stem-cell-transplant dosing, seizure-workup or progressive-neurology deferral, or side-effect treatment as household rules. Fever deferral is the household line; other precautions stay with the clinician.
- Not a household week-plan.
