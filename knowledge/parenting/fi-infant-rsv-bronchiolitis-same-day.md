# Finland — infant bronchiolitis is usually RSV; under-3-month feeding or breathing trouble is same-day care

## Guidance

- Bronchiolitis (ilmatiehyttulehdus) is small-airway inflammation in children **under 1 year**, most often **RSV**, and often peaks in **winter epidemics**. Other viruses (including rhinovirus) can cause it.
- The hardest age is **1–6 months**. Infants **under 3 months** and those born **preterm** are a special risk group; a large share of under-3-month cases need hospital care.
- Take a small infant to a **doctor** for **fast breathing**, **worsening work of breathing**, or **poor feeding / sucking**.
- During epidemic season, any infant **under 6 months** — **especially under 3 months** — with **feeding and breathing trouble** should **always** be assessed. Even a mild RSV infection often becomes a hospital stay.
- Hospital care is typical: support breathing, clear mucus, give oxygen. That is **not** home treatment.
- This is the **under-3-month RSV / same-day-care** rule, not a week-plan and not the already-filed under-2-month fever / never-shake note.

## Rationale

Duodecim Terveyskirjasto’s living “Ilmatiehyttulehdus (bronkioliitti) lapsella” page (Lääkärikirja Duodecim, dated 11 Mar 2024) is standing Finnish public-health wording. The under-3-month / under-6-month same-day assessment line, the RSV winter-epidemic pattern, and “do not watch-and-wait at home when feeding and breathing both fail” hold if any single day’s headline is deleted.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: bronchiolitis is defined **under 1 year**; the same-day rule is **under 6 months**, **especially under 3 months** (child tag `age-2-8w` now). Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage**. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
- **Finnish Terveyskirjasto / Duodecim** wording. Neuvola, the health centre, the maternity hospital, or 112 wins on the day.
- Not a diagnosis, an RSV test protocol, or a reason to skip the [under-2-month fever](./fi-infant-crying-fever-never-shake.md) rule.
- Do **not** apply the page’s pregnancy vaccine or newborn antibody injection as a household schedule — the page says national recommendations are still being prepared.
- Do **not** treat hospital mucus-clearing or oxygen as DIY home care.
- Not the fever / never-shake note and not a household week-plan.
