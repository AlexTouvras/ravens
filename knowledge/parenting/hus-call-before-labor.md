# HUS — always call 09 471 71500 before going in; go-in when contractions are under 10 minutes

## Guidance

- Birth starts with contractions or waters breaking. **Always call** the centralized synnyttäjien puhelinpalvelu (**09 471 71500**) before travelling in — do not self-present.
- A midwife triages and routes to Naistenklinikka, Espoo (Jorvi from 1 Sep 2026 09:00), or Hyvinkää using the parent's preference, medical criteria, and bed capacity.
- Plan to go in when contractions are regular **under 10 minutes** apart, last about a minute, and are so strong you must focus only on the contraction.
- Call **112** for very heavy bleeding, or continuous severe pain with bleeding or an urge to push.
- Hospital routing on the living HUS page: Espoo takes births from week **32+** and moves those births to Jorvi's new ward building from **1 Sep 2026 at 09:00**; Hyvinkää from week **35+**; Naistenklinikka covers HUS-area risk pregnancies and very preterm births.

## Rationale

HUS's living synnytys page is current Finnish-hospital protocol for a Helsinki-region household still in the late-pregnancy / labor window. The call-first rule, contraction timing, and 112 red flags are standing go-in checklist items — they hold if any single day's headline is deleted. The Espoo → Jorvi location change is on the same official page as the new routing fact from 1 Sep 2026.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Age window: `late-pregnancy` / `labor` only. Re-check [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) **Current stage** — a human sets `Child born`, not this note.
- **HUS catchment only** (Naistenklinikka / Espoo–Jorvi / Hyvinkää). Do not apply the phone number or routing to other Finnish hospital districts (TYKS, TAYS, OYS, KYS, …).
- Not a diagnosis or a substitute for the midwife on 09 471 71500 — waters breaking without strong contractions, reduced fetal movement, or other red flags still go through that line (or 112 when the page says so).
- The Jorvi move is a location change from 1 Sep 2026 09:00; if HUS later updates hospital names or the phone number, supersede this note rather than guessing.
