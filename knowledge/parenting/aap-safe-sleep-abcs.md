# AAP safe-sleep ABCs for the newborn's first days home

## Guidance

- Baby sleeps **alone**, **on the back**, on a firm, flat, non-inclined surface (crib or bassinet) — the "ABCs."
- **Room-share without bed-sharing**: baby's sleep surface stays in the parents' room, separate from the parents' bed, for at least the first 6 months.
- No soft bedding, pillows, blankets, bumpers, or loose objects in the sleep space.
- Avoid overheating; dress for room temperature rather than adding "just in case" layers.
- Set up the crib/bassinet in the parents' room **before the birth** so the sleep space is ready on arrival.

## Rationale

The AAP's safe-sleep ABCs are the current US pediatric consensus for reducing sleep-related infant death risk (including SIDS). The underlying policy was last revised in 2022 and is reaffirmed as active guidance in 2026 — a stable, professional-body baseline rather than a single news item.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Applies at `age-0-72h` and continues through at least 6 months for the room-sharing rule; re-check AAP guidance if a newer policy revision supersedes the 2022 wording.
- Does not cover feeding, jaundice, temperature, or other newborn-care topics — those need their own findings/notes.
- Not a substitute for pediatrician/neuvola guidance on a specific medical concern.
- Do not use this note to infer `Child born` or change **Current stage** in [`knowledge/parenting/README.md`](./README.md) — a human sets that after birth.
