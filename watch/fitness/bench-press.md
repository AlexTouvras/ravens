# Bench press — form check

## Why this clip

Jeff Nippard technique Tuesday: setup, execution, common mistakes. 12:14 — long for a glance, but the setup section is the useful part. Confirmed via watch URL 2026-08-17.

## Cues to steal

- Upper back tight, slight arch, feet planted; unrack without losing that.
- Bar path is not a straight drop — touch lower chest, press back toward the rack.
- Leave reps in reserve; this block is maintenance, not a max.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Ignore “huge bench” / powerlifting meet cues if they fight a sore shoulder or tomorrow’s run.
- Not a substitute for a spotter on heavy singles — we should not be there at RPE 7 × 8 anyway.
- Shoulder pain: stop.
