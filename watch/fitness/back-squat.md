# Back squat — form check

## Why this clip

Dr. Aaron Horschig (DPT) on Squat University. Side-on demo, 7:38, matches the coach-library back squat. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-08-17.

## Cues to steal

- Brace before you unrack; keep the torso quiet.
- Knees track over the toes; do not collapse inward.
- Depth is yours — stop before the lumbar rounds (butt wink).

## Limits / do not apply when

- Not a rehab plan for current back or knee pain — stop and get a clinician if it hurts.
- Pregnancy: use ACOG notes under `knowledge/fitness/`; this is a general barbell pattern, not prenatal programming.
- Title is bait (“NO BACK PAIN”); use the demo, not the promise.
