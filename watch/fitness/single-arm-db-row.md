# Single-arm DB row — form check

## Why this clip

Jeff Nippard Technique Tuesday: bench-supported single-arm dumbbell row with side and front camera. 7:23. Matches the coach-library single-arm DB row. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-08-23.

## Cues to steal

- Hand and knee on the bench; other foot wide. Torso near parallel (about 0–20°), not upright on the rack.
- Start with a stretch (scapula protracted); drag the dumbbell back toward the hip in an arc, elbow close.
- A little torso rotation is fine; a full “bro row” yank is not. Control the negative.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Title is bait (“Cheating”). Use the moderate-momentum cue, not an excuse to heave.
- Shoulder or low-back pain: stop. This is not a rehab plan.
- Pregnancy: use ACOG notes under `knowledge/fitness/`; this is a general dumbbell pattern.
