# Plyo push-up — form check

## Why this clip

Scott Herman (NASM-CPT / ACE) in his How-To series. Dedicated plyometric / clap push-up, 2:26, camera on the movement — not a Short. Matches the coach-library **plyo push-ups**. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-08-30.

NASM’s own form clip exists but is 10 seconds; skip Shorts-only when this longer tutorial is live.

## Cues to steal

- Start in a tight push-up plank; lower under control, then explode so the hands leave the floor.
- Clap only if you still own the landing — the airtime is the point, not the clap.
- Catch with soft elbows and a quiet torso; hips stay in line (no pike, no sag).
- Cannot leave the floor yet: regular push-up, then knees, until the lockout is crisp.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Wrist, elbow, or shoulder pain: stop. This is not a rehab plan.
- Not a 100-push-up challenge or “destroy your chest” clip.
- Pregnancy: use ACOG notes under `knowledge/fitness/`; do not layer plyo push-ups onto prenatal programming.
- Ignore Tough Mudder / obstacle packaging if you land on Herman’s other circuits — this note is the plyo push-up only.
