# Split squat — form check

## Why this clip

Squat University, 5:20, clear front-leg demo. The coach library says **split squat** (rear foot can stay on the floor). This clip is the rear-foot-elevated (Bulgarian) version — closest durable tutorial; cues still transfer.

## Cues to steal

- Most of the load on the front leg; rear foot is a kickstand (or a light elevates).
- Front knee tracks over the toes; torso stays stacked.
- Shorten the stance if the rear hip cramps or the front heel lifts.

## Limits / do not apply when

- If the session is a **floor** split squat, ignore the rear-foot box height; keep the same front-leg pattern.
- Skip or regress if the front knee or rear hip complains.
- Not ATG / knees-over-toes challenge content.
