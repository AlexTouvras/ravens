# Nordic curl — form check

## Why this clip

E3 Rehab (PT clinic) covers setup, the eccentric, regressions, and a machine curl alternative — useful because the library allows “Nordic curl **or** hamstring curl”. 10:30. Confirmed via watch URL 2026-08-17.

## Cues to steal

- Anchor the heels; hips stay extended; lower with control.
- If you cannot finish the eccentric, use a band, a shorter range, or a lying hamstring curl.
- Stop short of a face-plant; this is maintenance RPE, not a max eccentric.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Fresh hamstring strain or current posterior-chain pain — skip.
- During the 5K block this is accessory work; do not chase failure before a quality run.
- GHD / partner-held setups only if you actually have them.
