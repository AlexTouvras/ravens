# Drop jump / broad jump — form check

## Why this clip

Dane Miller (S&C, Garage Strength) coaches the **broad jump** on-camera with athletes: countermovement, arm sweep, high heel recovery, stick the landing. 10:17. Library name is **drop jumps or broad jumps** — this is the horizontal half. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-08-30.

Backup (depth-jump library clip, not a second file): Catalyst Athletics — [Depth Jump | Olympic Weightlifting Exercise Library](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GeN0S3XCZnM) (1:20).

## Cues to steal

- Strong countermovement: arms sweep back, torso almost in line with the hips, then project 40–45°.
- Aggressive arms forward; **high heel recovery** in the air (heels that stay low leak distance).
- Knees come through; arms cycle back under; reach to a flat-foot, stuck landing.

## Limits / do not apply when

- This clip is a **standing broad jump**, not a box drop-jump / depth jump. If the session is drop jumps, step off a low box and rebound with short ground contact — do not copy the long-jump arm cycle onto a drop jump.
- Drop/depth jumps are high-impact. Keep the box modest; stop if a knee, Achilles, or landing is noisy or asymmetric.
- Combine-distance chasing is not the W6 accessory. Quality landings over max feet.
- Pregnancy: use ACOG notes under `knowledge/fitness/`; skip reactive jumps unless a clinician cleared them.
