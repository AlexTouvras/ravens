# Calf raise — form check

## Why this clip

Tim Keeley (physio, Physio REHAB) demos the calf / heel-raise from two-foot isometric through single-leg off a step. 8:01. Visible demo; PT channel. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-08-23.

## Cues to steal

- Straight knee for gastroc; bent knee if you want more soleus. Don’t bounce.
- Full up, pause; lower under control. First-metatarsal contact — don’t roll to the outside.
- Floor first; add a step only when you can lock out and control the descent.

## Limits / do not apply when

- This is an **Achilles / calf-rehab progression**, not a “grow your calves” session. On a healthy W6 accessory set, start at the two-foot or single-leg floor raise — skip the post-boot isometrics unless you are actually rehabbing.
- Fresh calf strain, Achilles pain, or a flare after the 5K work — stop and get a clinician.
- Pregnancy: use ACOG notes under `knowledge/fitness/` if loading changes.
