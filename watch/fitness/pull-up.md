# Pull-up — form check

## Why this clip

Jeff Nippard (BSc biochemistry, natural bodybuilding) — technique plus common mistakes, 8:33. Library allows pull-ups **or** lat pulldown; use a pulldown if you cannot control full pull-ups at RPE 7.

## Cues to steal

- Dead hang to chin over the bar (or chest toward the bar) without kipping.
- Think elbows down/back; avoid shrugging into the ears.
- If you miss reps, switch to a pulldown with the same elbow path.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Shoulder pain, recent strain, or a grind that wrecks tomorrow’s run — stop.
- “Wide back” in the title is hypertrophy marketing; we are on maintenance during the 5K block.
- Not a first-pull-up program.
