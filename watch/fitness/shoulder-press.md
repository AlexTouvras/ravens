# Shoulder press — form check

## Why this clip

Jeff Nippard Technique Tuesday on the standing barbell overhead press — side-on bar path, setup, and the usual mistakes. 7:59. Library name is **shoulder press**; this is the barbell OHP. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-08-23.

## Cues to steal

- Unrack, two to three steps back, feet about shoulder-width, glutes on, brace.
- Press the bar straight up; head tilts back to clear, then through. Lock out over mid-foot.
- Elbows about 45° — not flared wide, not jammed straight forward. Full stop at the chest; no bounce.

## Limits / do not apply when

- If the session is a **dumbbell** or machine press, keep the same vertical path; ignore the rack walkout.
- Shoulder pain or a grind that wrecks tomorrow’s run — stop.
- Pregnancy: use ACOG notes under `knowledge/fitness/`; this is not prenatal programming.
- Ignore the “bigger shoulders” / program-sale closer.
