# Romanian deadlift — form check

## Why this clip

Same PT channel as the squat note. Hip-hinge demo with a clear bar path, 7:12. Confirmed via watch URL 2026-08-17.

## Cues to steal

- Soft knees, then push the hips back; the bar stays close.
- Stop when the hamstrings load and the spine is still neutral — not when the plates hit the floor.
- Unhinge by driving the hips forward, not by yanking the chest up.

## Limits / do not apply when

- This is an RDL, not a conventional deadlift from the floor.
- Not a fix for current low-back pain.
- Pregnancy: hinge patterns need a clinician or ACOG-aligned plan; do not grind this at RPE 7+ if the belly changes the lever.
