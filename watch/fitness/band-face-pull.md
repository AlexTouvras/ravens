# Band face pull — form check

## Why this clip

Dr. John Rusin (DPT, CSCS) demos a **banded** face-pull / pull-apart at eye height, then the cable-rope version. 2:42. Matches the library “band face pulls or explosive band pulls”. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-08-23.

Backup (longer band setup, not a second file): [How To PROPERLY Do Face Pulls For Prehab & Shoulder Health](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CU4Xc2qlLC0) — Citizen Athletics, 6:32.

## Cues to steal

- Band at the eyes; hands about 4–6 inches apart, palms down; pull back **and** apart.
- Ribcage down; slight external rotation at the end. Don’t shrug.
- Light enough that the hands win the race to the face — not a heavy row.

## Limits / do not apply when

- The last variation (handless band on a cable) needs a cable stack we may not have. Use the first band demo.
- “Explosive band pulls” in the library are a faster cousin; do not yank into the end range if the shoulder is cranky.
- Shoulder pain: stop.
- Ignore the Bodybuilding.com packaging; the cues are the PT’s.
