# Breastmilk storage — handle it, then put it away

## Why this clip

Global Health Media’s English breastfeeding-series film on handling expressed milk and storing it for a later feed. 3:21. Separate from the hand-expression demo. The publisher says the camera shows handling and storage so the milk does not make the baby sick. Aimed first at mothers in the developing world, and marked as useful more widely. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-10-04.

## Cues to steal

- Handle the milk so it does not make the baby sick.
- Store it for a later feed rather than leaving it sitting out.
- Expression itself stays in [hand-expression](./hand-expression.md). This clip is what you do with the milk after it is in the container.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Do **not** copy a storage clock from this film into a Finnish kitchen. The description gives no hour chart, and the film’s first audience is a warm-climate setting. **Neuvola** sets fridge, freezer, and thaw times on the day.
- Not a reason to discard milk a clinician said to keep. Not a pump-settings guide.
- Mastitis emptying rules stay in [fi-postpartum-mastitis-empty-12h](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-postpartum-mastitis-empty-12h.md). Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
