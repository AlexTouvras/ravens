# Breast pain — engorgement, blocked duct, mastitis

## Why this clip

Global Health Media’s English breastfeeding-series demo of the three common causes of breast pain: engorgement, a blocked duct, and infection (mastitis). 7:55. Same public-health channel as the latch note. Visible signs and what the parent can do at home. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-09-13.

## Cues to steal

- A sharply bordered tender red patch can be a blocked duct. Feed from the sore side first if it is comfortable.
- Keep feeding from the inflamed breast unless a clinician says otherwise. Milk stays suitable.
- Cool pack between feeds for swelling. Hand expression is the skill in [hand-expression](./hand-expression.md).

## Limits / do not apply when

- **Finnish Terveyskirjasto / Duodecim** wins on the day: **no fever** → empty the breast; **high fever** or **no ease in 12 hours** of frequent emptying → doctor / health centre the same day — [fi-postpartum-mastitis-empty-12h](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-postpartum-mastitis-empty-12h.md).
- Not a diagnosis, an antibiotic plan, or a drug list. An abscess (ongoing fever plus a separate tender lump) is a doctor visit.
- Burning-nipple pain can be Candida — check the baby’s mouth — [fi-infant-oral-thrush-dummy](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-infant-oral-thrush-dummy.md).
- Not the lochia / endometritis fever split. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
