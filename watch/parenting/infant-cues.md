# Infant cues — sleepy, then the interest fades

## Why this clip

Global Health Media’s English film in the USAID Responsive Care and Early Learning series, made with JSI. 12:01. The publisher page embeds this id: https://globalhealthmedia.org/videos/universal-baby-cues-english/. Nurturing Care lists the same film at 12 min 01 sec and names the ten scenes: https://nurturing-care.org/universal-baby-cues/. The camera shows caregivers with babies in Ghana and the Kyrgyz Republic, not a talking head. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-11-15.

## Cues to steal

- Scene 1 is a 2-month-old signalling sleepy. Scene 2 is a 2-month-old engaged with a parent, then losing interest. Those two scenes are the match for this stage.
- Watch the face, the eyes, the body, and the sounds. The page’s own questions are hungry, full, sleepy, interested, and lost interest.
- When interest drops, stop the game. Answer the cue in front of you.
- Early hunger for this household stays the Finnish list: mouth opening, fist to the mouth, sucking the fingers, before a full cry. This film’s hunger scene is a 4-month-old.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Scenes 3 and 4 are 4-month-olds. Scenes 5–10 run from 6 months to 18 months (play, a book, finished eating, wanting to go outside). They are not this stage. Do not turn them into a plan.
- The YouTube description addresses health workers. The pictures are ordinary caregiver-and-baby moments. Neuvola wins on the day.
- Not sleep training. A sleepy cue is a reason to offer rest, on the back, in a clear cot — [newborn-safer-sleep](./newborn-safer-sleep.md).
- Crying, fever under 2 months, or a baby who seems ill still needs a clinician — [fi-infant-crying-fever-never-shake](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-infant-crying-fever-never-shake.md).
- Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage. Do not compute week of life.
