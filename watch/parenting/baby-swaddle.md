# Swaddling a baby — thin wrap for startle

## Why this clip

Väestöliitto Hyvä kysymys film, 2:50. The publisher page embeds this id and says the clip shows how to swaddle. Midwife Juulia Ukkonen-Wallmeroth. The written steps are for a newborn or a very active baby who startles and wakes themselves. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-11-01. Page: hyvakysymys.fi/video/vauvan-kapalointi.

## Cues to steal

- Use a thin, large cloth. Spread it flat and lay the baby along the top edge.
- Put one hand on the chest, draw that side over the arm, and tuck it under the body. Put the other hand on the chest and draw the second side fairly snug over that arm and under the body. The hands can also cross with no cloth between them.
- Lift the hanging tail so the baby bends their knees, bring the cloth up to the chest, and tuck one side then the other under the body.
- The page says this wrap can be used through about the first 3–4 months, then stopped so the baby can find a hand or fingers while settling.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Sleep stays [newborn-safer-sleep](./newborn-safer-sleep.md): back, clear cot, not too hot, face uncovered. The page names a duvet, a sheet, or a duvet cover as a possible wrap cloth, and it allows folding a small baby's head in with about 10 cm of fabric above the head. Those are not extra cot bedding, and a covered face is not the sleep setup.
- The film's own leg step is a bend at the knees. Do not pull the legs straight inside the wrap.
- Stop by about 3–4 months on this film's line. Hands stay free for a feed. Layer counts stay [dress-baby](./dress-baby.md).
- This is a startle wrap, not a sleep-training method. The same site's article about leaving a baby alone to settle is not this clip.
- Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
