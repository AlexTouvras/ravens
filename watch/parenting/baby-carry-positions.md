# Baby carry positions — vary the hold

## Why this clip

Länsi-Uudenmaan hyvinvointialue (LUVN) film of several ways to carry a baby in arms. 7:04. The bilingual description says the film shows the positions, that varying them lowers the risk of neck, shoulder, and back trouble for the adult, and that different positions also support the child's sensorimotor development. Regional physiotherapy film, not a sling-brand advert. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-10-18.

## Cues to steal

- Learn more than one arm carry, and switch during the day.
- Varying the hold is for the adult's neck, shoulders, and back, and it gives the baby more than one position to feel.

## Limits / do not apply when

- A sling or soft carrier still needs the TICKS airway check — [baby-sling](./baby-sling.md). These are arm carries.
- Picking the baby up off the floor, and supporting a wobbly head, is [baby-handling](./baby-handling.md). The four safety checks while you hold are [hold-newborn](./hold-newborn.md).
- Awake tummy time on a chest, lap, or floor is [tummy-time](./tummy-time.md), not a carry.
- Neuvola wins if they teach a different set of holds. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
