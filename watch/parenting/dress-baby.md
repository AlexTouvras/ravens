# Dressing a baby — over-the-head body

## Why this clip

Väestöliitto Hyvä kysymys film, 2:09. The description says the clip shows putting on a body that goes over the head, on a changing table, a bed, or the floor. The handling line is calm, clear holds, telling the baby what is happening, and keeping a hand on them. Same publisher as the handling film. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-10-25.

## Cues to steal

- Say what you are about to do. Rest a hand on the baby. Tap the arm or leg gently before you dress or undress it.
- Over-the-head body: ease one arm out, then the other. If the neck has buttons, roll from side to side to open them, then draw the body over the head.
- If the shirt over the face upsets them, gather their hands on the chest, put your hands over theirs, find their eyes, and talk.
- Fasteners on the back: roll onto the tummy, open them, then roll back through the side. Finish the snaps between the legs, then trousers and socks.

## Limits / do not apply when

- A roll onto the tummy is to free a shirt, not a sleep position and not tummy time. Back to sleep stays [newborn-safer-sleep](./newborn-safer-sleep.md). Awake tummy time is [tummy-time](./tummy-time.md). Neck support stays [baby-handling](./baby-handling.md).
- The same midwife's wrap-shirt film `jvmyEtJeUpk` (1:45) is under the length floor, so it is not the primary. Its description is the layer line: a couple of layers indoors, a third layer outside in spring and autumn, a fourth in winter. It also shows rolling a wrap sleeve and drawing the arm through. Do not copy a brand-body advert onto this skill.
- The neck-warmth check (nape, not hands and feet) stays in [fi-newborn-first-days-care](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-newborn-first-days-care.md). Do not add a hat indoors to "fix" cool hands.
