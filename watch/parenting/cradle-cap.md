# Cradle cap — mild shampoo, soft brush, do not pick

## Why this clip

St. Louis Children’s Hospital how-to for yellowish scalp scale (cradle cap / taliköhnä). 2:28. Paediatric-hospital camera, not a shampoo haul. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-09-27.

Backup (AAP talking-head, Dr DeWayne Pursley, 2:25 — do **not** use baby oil): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MjwqOY7AA18

## Cues to steal

- Cradle cap is common and not poor hygiene. Wash the scalp with a mild baby shampoo more often, then loosen scale with a soft brush.
- Do not pick or scrape until it bleeds.
- AAP wording in the backup: petroleum jelly or ointment can help; **baby oil** can make scale build up.

## Limits / do not apply when

- **Finnish Terveyskirjasto / Duodecim** wins on the day: cradle cap is harmless and almost always gone by **six months**; neuvola can give the shampoo / oil how-to — [fi-infant-skin-navel-diaper-rash](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-infant-skin-navel-diaper-rash.md). Use neuvola’s product advice if it differs from the US clip.
- Not a diagnosis. Spreading redness, blisters, pus, or a baby who seems ill is neuvola / a doctor. Fever under 2 months always needs a clinician.
- Not milia, baby acne, navel-stump care, or a product haul. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
