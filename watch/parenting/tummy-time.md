# Tummy time — chest, lap, then floor

## Why this clip

Pathways.org (pediatric PT/OT-reviewed nonprofit) demos four awake, supervised positions: tummy-to-chest, eye-level smile on the floor, lap soothe, and tummy-down carry. 3:08. Matches the week-3 Finnish rule — start on the parent’s chest or lap, then the floor — not a minute-count challenge. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-09-06.

Backup (same channel, longer tips): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I-Fhn7LPsEs (10:25).

## Cues to steal

- Awake and watched. Chest or lap first if the floor is too hard; floor when they tolerate it.
- Short spells. Stop when they fuss; try again later. Quality beats duration this week.
- Elbows in, face clear. Never a sleep position — back to sleep stays in [newborn-safer-sleep](./newborn-safer-sleep.md).

## Limits / do not apply when

- **Finnish Terveyskirjasto** quality-over-duration wording wins this week. Pathways’ “about an hour a day by 3 months” and AAP’s 15–30 minutes/day by 7 weeks are later volume targets — [SIG-20260906-001](../../signals/SIG-20260906-001.md) — not tonight’s quota.
- Unsupervised tummy time, or a baby who cannot breathe freely, is out of scope.
- Not medical advice for torticollis, plagiocephaly treatment, or a baby who cannot lift the head at all — that is neuvola.
