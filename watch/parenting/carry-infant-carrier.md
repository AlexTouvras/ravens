# Carry an infant carrier — lift

## Why this clip

Länsi-Uudenmaan hyvinvointialue (LUVN) physiotherapy film on lifting and carrying the infant car carrier. 2:55. Swedish narration with a Finnish description: a few tips for a safe, ergonomic lift and carry so the load on the back and shoulders is smaller. Regional wellbeing-services film, listed with the other baby films on the LUVN rehab-video page. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-10-18.

## Cues to steal

- Use the lift and the carry the film shows. The point is a safer load on the back and shoulders, not a faster walk to the car.
- The carrier is heavy with a baby in it. Change sides and put it down rather than hitching it on one shoulder for the whole trip.

## Limits / do not apply when

- How the baby is buckled, and which way the carrier faces in the car, is [infant-car-seat](./infant-car-seat.md) and the carrier manual. This film is the lift.
- Arm carries of the baby, without the shell, are [baby-carry-positions](./baby-carry-positions.md). A fabric sling is [baby-sling](./baby-sling.md).
- Pain that does not ease, or a scar that pulls, goes to neuvola or the postpartum check. This is not a rehab plan.
- Neuvola wins if they teach a different lift. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
