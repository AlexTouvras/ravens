# Infant choking — back blows, then chest thrusts

## Why this clip

British Red Cross training film, 4:25. It is the YouTube film on their baby-choking page. Timestamps in the description: story, then step-by-step from 0:39, key steps at 3:53. The description says the film teaches how many back blows and chest thrusts to give. The page that embeds this film is the written count. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-10-25.

## Cues to steal

- A choking baby cannot cry, make noise, cough, or breathe. Newborns can choke on milk, mucus, or vomit.
- Up to five back blows: face-down along your thigh, head lower than the bottom, head supported, firm blows between the shoulder blades. Gentler on a smaller baby.
- If still choking, up to five chest thrusts: face up, two fingers in the middle of the chest just below the nipples, sharp pushes downward.
- Still choking: call for help and repeat those cycles until they breathe or help arrives.

## Limits / do not apply when

- In Finland call **112**, not the 999 on the British page. The dispatcher wins if they give a different count.
- No abdominal thrusts and no Heimlich on a baby. That is the child and adult film. Do not hold them upside down by the feet.
- Do not finger-sweep a mouth you cannot see into. Pluck only an object you can see and can lift out with fingertips.
- If they become unresponsive and stop breathing, switch to [infant-cpr](./infant-cpr.md). A blocked nose is [blocked-nose](./blocked-nose.md), not this film.
- A shorter British Red Cross cut is `ePodw7L_mFM` (2:44). Suomen Punainen Risti `5RNPF-glATE` is 0:43, under the length floor. Do not mix those counts with a child film.
- Not a diagnosis. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
