# Handle a baby — side lift

## Why this clip

Väestöliitto Hyvä Kysymys film on handling a small baby. 2:03. The publisher description is the cue list: a gentle firm hold, neck support while head control is still uncertain, a lift that comes via the side, gathering the limbs toward the middle, and keeping the baby close while you shift your own weight. Finnish family-organisation film, not a hold hack. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-10-18.

## Cues to steal

- Hold gently and firmly. Support the neck when head control, or your own hands, still feel uncertain.
- Lift via the side, from a lap or the floor. From there, bring the baby in against you — over the shoulder, the baby's armpits on your shoulder, your hands behind the baby's shoulder line.
- A restless baby on the side or the back can be gathered toward a curl, limbs toward the middle. Some babies also settle with a gentle hold around the head.
- Keep the baby close. Shift your weight, or lean a little forward or back, until the hold matches your body.

## Limits / do not apply when

- "Head support when lying" on the 2–3 month development note is a milestone. It is not permission to let the head flop while you lift.
- Several arm carries, and switching them, are [baby-carry-positions](./baby-carry-positions.md). The four safety checks while you walk are [hold-newborn](./hold-newborn.md).
