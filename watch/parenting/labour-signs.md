# Labour signs — when to go in

## Why this clip

Official NHS trust, 4:13, early labour without influencer framing. Applies at **late-pregnancy** until you are in the unit.

## Cues to steal

- Know the difference between early/latent labour and “time to go in” as **your Finnish unit** explained it.
- Waters, regular contractions, bleeding, reduced movements: call the number they gave you (or 112), not a YouTube comment.

## Limits / do not apply when

- This is **UK** maternity advice. Finnish timing, neuvola, and the hospital/home-coming plan you were given win.
- Not a diagnosis. Not a substitute for the midwife on call.
- Do not invent a date of birth from this clip.
