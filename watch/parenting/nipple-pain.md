# Nipple pain — usually a shallow latch

## Why this clip

Global Health Media’s English breastfeeding-series film on nipple pain. 6:51. The publisher’s description: pain is common but not normal, often when breastfeeding is new, and usually because the baby is not attaching deeply. The film shows how that happens and what the mother can change. Mother-facing camera, not a cream advert. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-10-04.

## Cues to steal

- Nipple pain at the start is common. It is not something to endure as “normal”.
- The usual cause in this film is a shallow attachment, not a mystery diagnosis.
- Change the latch. The attachment demo is [newborn-latch](./newborn-latch.md). Positions are [breastfeeding-positions](./breastfeeding-positions.md).

## Limits / do not apply when

- Breast pain (engorgement, a blocked duct, mastitis) is a different film and a different Finnish rule — [breast-pain](./breast-pain.md), [fi-postpartum-mastitis-empty-12h](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-postpartum-mastitis-empty-12h.md).
- Burning nipple pain can be Candida. Check the baby’s mouth. That is not this clip — [fi-infant-oral-thrush-dummy](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-infant-oral-thrush-dummy.md).
- Not a cream plan and not a reason to stop feeding unless a clinician says so. The film is aimed first at mothers in the developing world; neuvola wins on the day. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
