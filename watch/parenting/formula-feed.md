## Cues to steal

- Make the feed when the baby is ready to eat. Boil fresh tap water and use it within 30 minutes so the UK method still has water hot enough (at least 70°C) to kill bacteria in the powder.
- Clean the surface and wash hands. Put the water in the sterilised bottle first. Add loosely filled, level scoops from that tin only — the scoop that came with the powder.
- Swirl to dissolve. Hard shaking adds air. Cool the bottle under cold running water. Test on the inside of the wrist. It should feel warm, not hot.
- Pour away what the baby does not take, as soon as the feed is finished.

## Limits / do not apply when

- **Finnish neuvola, the tin, and Ruokavirasto** win if they differ from this UK 70°C / tap-water method. Do not copy the page’s UK vitamin line. Finnish dosing is **10 µg/day from 2 weeks**, reduced when formula is **500 ml/day or more** — [fi-infant-vitamin-d-from-2-weeks](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-infant-vitamin-d-from-2-weeks.md).
- A dip in breast-milk supply is repaired with more frequent feeds, not an automatic formula top-up — [fi-infant-milk-supply-dummy](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-infant-milk-supply-dummy.md).
- Not a brand choice, a prep-machine recommendation, or a volume target. Wash and sterilise first — [bottle-sterilise](./bottle-sterilise.md). Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
