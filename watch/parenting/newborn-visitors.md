# Newborn visitors — hands, and no kisses

## Why this clip

Lullaby Trust animation for people coming to see a newborn. 2:07. Illustrated scenes, not a talking head. The publisher description states the rule and the acronym: wash hands before touching a newborn, do not kiss a young baby, THANKS — Think Hands And No Kisses. Same charity as the safer-sleep film. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-11-22.

## Cues to steal

- Visitors wash their hands before they touch the baby.
- Visitors do not kiss a young baby.
- THANKS: Think Hands And No Kisses.

## Limits / do not apply when

- **Lullaby Trust** visitor line. Neuvola or the maternity ward’s own infection advice wins on the day.
- The film is about **visitors**. It is not a rule that a parent cannot kiss their own baby during ordinary care.
- A baby who seems ill, or **fever under 2 months**, still needs a clinician — [fi-infant-crying-fever-never-shake](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-infant-crying-fever-never-shake.md). This clip is not a household isolation plan.
- Not a diagnosis, and not a reason to skip feeds, vitamin D, or the safer-sleep cot — [newborn-safer-sleep](./newborn-safer-sleep.md).
- Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage. Do not compute week of life.
