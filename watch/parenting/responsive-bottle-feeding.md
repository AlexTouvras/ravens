# Responsive bottle-feeding — cues, pace, do not force

## Cues to steal

- Start from hunger cues. Crying is late.
- Prepare the feed before you settle in. Sterile bottle first — [bottle-sterilise](./bottle-sterilise.md) and, for formula, [formula-feed](./formula-feed.md).
- Let the baby set the pace. When they need a break, pause instead of tipping the bottle steeper.
- Burp. Stop when they have had enough. Do not finish the bottle for them.

## Limits / do not apply when

- **Finnish Terveyskirjasto / THL** feeding wording wins on the day. An established milk supply is repaired by more frequent breastfeeds, not an automatic bottle — [fi-infant-milk-supply-dummy](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-infant-milk-supply-dummy.md).
- Wind after a feed is the skill in [infant-wind](./infant-wind.md). Spit-up with steady growth is common; projectile vomiting at 3 weeks–2 months needs a doctor — [fi-infant-spit-up-pyloric-stenosis](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-infant-spit-up-pyloric-stenosis.md).
- Not a schedule, a sleep-training method, or a prop. Never leave the baby alone with a bottle. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
