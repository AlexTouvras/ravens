# Hand expression — empty for comfort

## Why this clip

Global Health Media’s English breastfeeding-series demo of hand expression: wash, C-hold at the edge of the areola, compress-and-release, change sides when the flow slows. 7:30. Camera shows the skill. Useful when a breast is full, a latch is hard, or the Finnish mastitis rule is “empty first”. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-09-13.

## Cues to steal

- Wash hands. Wide-mouth clean container. Relax and think of the baby before the first drops.
- Thumb and fingers opposite each other behind the nipple — do not slide or pinch the nipple.
- Compress, release, collect. Rotate around the breast; switch sides when the stream slows.

## Limits / do not apply when

- **Finnish Terveyskirjasto / Duodecim** mastitis rule wins on the day: empty if there is **no fever**; high fever or no ease in **12 hours** needs a doctor — [fi-postpartum-mastitis-empty-12h](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-postpartum-mastitis-empty-12h.md). This clip is the skill, not the 12-hour threshold.
- Not a pump-settings guide and not a reason to discard milk. Keep feeding unless a clinician says otherwise.
- First-hours “no latch in two hours → hand-express” is a passed window — [fi-first-latch-hand-express](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-first-latch-hand-express.md). Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
