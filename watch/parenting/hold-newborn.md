# Hold a baby — four Ss

## Why this clip

Boston Children's Hospital Injury Prevention film on holding and carrying a baby. 6:28. The hospital page and the video description list the same outline: the four Ss of safety (Support, Safe, Secure, and Sustainable), how holds change with age, and mistakes to avoid. Paediatric hospital film, not a parenting-influencer hold. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-10-11.

## Cues to steal

- Use the film's four checks: Support, Safe, Secure, and Sustainable.
- Watch the age-specific holds and the mistakes section in the same film. Do not paste in a different "four S" list from a sleep book or an attachment book.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Never shake a baby. Crying that will not settle is [infant-soothing](./infant-soothing.md) and [fi-infant-crying-fever-never-shake](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-infant-crying-fever-never-shake.md), not a tighter grip.
- Everyday carrying, not a comfort hold for a blood test or vaccine. A sling is a different film — [baby-sling](./baby-sling.md).
- "Head support when lying" on the 2–3 month development note is a milestone, not permission to let the head flop while you walk. If a hold leaves the head or neck unsupported, it fails Support.
- Neuvola wins if they teach a different carry. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
