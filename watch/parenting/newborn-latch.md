# Newborn latch / attaching at the breast

## Why this clip

Global Health Media’s breastfeeding series is the usual public-health demo (wide gape, baby to breast, not breast to baby). 10:27. Still in play at week 3–4 for positioning. Same `WATCH-` id; URL re-confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-09-06.

## Cues to steal

- Baby’s body lined up; chin in; more areola visible above the lip than below.
- Pain that stays after the first seconds is a latch problem — get help (neuvola / midwife), do not “tough it out”.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Not a substitute for a lactation visit. Tongue-tie, mastitis, and supply issues need a person.
- Formula / mixed feeding is a valid plan; this clip does not argue otherwise.
- Finnish infant feeding-safety (honey, formula prep) stays under `knowledge/parenting/` text notes, not this video.
