## Cues to steal

- Start with this introduction, then use the body-area film for the part you are touching.
- The trust describes the set as soothing, relaxing touch. The stroke demo is the matching body clip, not a list of invented strokes.
- Finish with the after-care film.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Not a sleep-training method. Not a treatment for cradle cap, eczema, teething, colic, wind, reflux, or low mood. Cradle cap stays with [cradle-cap](./cradle-cap.md). Wind stays with [infant-wind](./infant-wind.md). Crying that will not settle stays with [infant-soothing](./infant-soothing.md).
- Do not massage a navel that is red and warm, or skin with blisters or pus — same-day care, [fi-infant-skin-navel-diaper-rash](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-infant-skin-navel-diaper-rash.md). Fever under 2 months still needs a doctor — [fi-infant-crying-fever-never-shake](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-infant-crying-fever-never-shake.md).
- Press lines about immune stimulation, weight gain, or curing teething are not household rules. A short bottom clip on the same page is under 2 minutes; use the longer tutorials above.
- Neuvola wins on the day. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
