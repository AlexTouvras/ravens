# Enough milk — swallows, wet nappies, alert feeds

## Why this clip

Global Health Media’s English breastfeeding-series demo of three intake signs: stools and urine, deep jaw-drop swallows versus shallow suckling, and an alert baby who feeds often. 6:01. Same public-health channel as latch / positions. Camera compares a drinking baby with one who is only suckling. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-09-27.

## Cues to steal

- After the first week: look for regular wet nappies and loose yellow stools, not meconium.
- A drinking baby’s jaw pauses and drops with each swallow. Rapid shallow sucks with no drop often mean poor attachment — go back to [newborn-latch](./newborn-latch.md).
- A baby who is getting enough is alert at the breast and usually comes off satisfied. Sleepy after only shallow sucks may wake hungry again soon.

## Limits / do not apply when

- **Finnish Terveyskirjasto / THL** wins on the day: established supply does not stop suddenly; check intake by **weighing**; from the **fourth day**, at least **4–6 unmistakably wet nappies** — [fi-infant-milk-supply-dummy](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-infant-milk-supply-dummy.md). This clip’s “six to eight wees” is GHM wording, not the Finnish count.
- First-days meconium / day-4 warning is a passed window. Do not re-hunt labour or the first latch.
- Wait on a dummy until latch and weight are rising. Contact neuvola if worried about intake. Not a formula-vs-breast argument. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
