# Infant temperature — armpit or rectal, never oral

## Why this clip

NYU Langone Medical Center parent-education demo of two ways to take a young infant’s temperature (armpit and rectal). 5:09. Hospital camera shows the skill. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-09-27.

## Cues to steal

- Digital thermometer only. Do not use glass. Do not take an oral reading in a young infant.
- Armpit: tip on skin in the mid-axilla; hold the arm snug against the body until the beep.
- Ask neuvola which method they want on the day. Wait after a bath before you measure.

## Limits / do not apply when

- **Finnish Terveyskirjasto / THL** wins on the day: **fever under 2 months always needs a doctor** — [fi-infant-crying-fever-never-shake](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-infant-crying-fever-never-shake.md). Do not wait on a US “100.4 °F” cut-off.
- Under-6-month (especially under 3 months) feeding **and** breathing trouble is same-day care — [fi-infant-rsv-bronchiolitis-same-day](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-infant-rsv-bronchiolitis-same-day.md).
- This clip is the measurement skill, not a diagnosis or a medicine dose. Neuvola, the health centre, or 112 wins. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
