# Navel care — clean and dry stump

## Why this clip

Tampere University Hospital (Tays) film, 2:08. The description says the newborn stump starts to dry and shrink and comes off within a week or two, and that the navel is kept clean and dry so it does not get infected. Finnish hospital demo of stump care, not a chlorhexidine program for health workers. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-10-25. Publisher line points at tays.fi/synnytys.

## Cues to steal

- Let the stump dry and shrink. The film says it comes off in about one to two weeks.
- Keep the navel clean and dry so it does not get infected. Follow the cleaning the film shows.

## Limits / do not apply when

- Do not use the Global Health Media cord film (`5xUY7SlGxfM`). That one is chlorhexidine for health workers. NHS `bdOue2hKzM8` (1:22) and Nemours `qFPA-4A_8wY` (1:40) stay under the length floor.
- Written Finnish home care is a dry cotton bud — [fi-newborn-first-days-care](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-newborn-first-days-care.md). This film states clean and dry. It does not name a product. Neuvola or the discharging ward wins if they show a different wipe.
- Redness and warmth around the navel, or blisters and pus, are same-day care — [fi-infant-skin-navel-diaper-rash](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-infant-skin-navel-diaper-rash.md). A smell is not a home disinfectant experiment.
- After a bath, dry the stump and the folds. Bathing itself is [infant-bath](./infant-bath.md).
- Not a diagnosis. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
