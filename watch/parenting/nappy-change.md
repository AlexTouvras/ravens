# Nappy change — clean, dry, air

## Why this clip

Newcastle upon Tyne Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust midwife-style demo of a nappy change (floor or safe flat surface, front-to-back wipe, nappy-free air). 3:36. Longer than the national NHS talking clip (1:43), so it clears the 2–12 minute how-to floor. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-09-13.

Backup (national NHS midwife, 1:43): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XEV-oQmGDto

## Cues to steal

- Change as soon as the nappy is wet or dirty. Have cotton wool + warm water (or fragrance-free wipes), a clean nappy, and a bag ready.
- Wipe **front to back**. Clean the folds. Pat dry.
- If it is warm enough, a short nappy-free spell. Thin barrier / zinc-oxide only on clean dry skin.

## Limits / do not apply when

- **Finnish Terveyskirjasto / Duodecim** wins on the day: water not soap, air baths, thin zinc-oxide; show neuvola if the rash **lasts over a week**, is **cratered**, or **spreads beyond the diaper area** — [fi-infant-skin-navel-diaper-rash](../../knowledge/parenting/fi-infant-skin-navel-diaper-rash.md).
- Never leave a baby on a raised changing table. Floor + mat is the safer default.
- Navel warmth, blisters, or pus is same-day care — not more cream. Fever under 2 months always needs a clinician.
- Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
