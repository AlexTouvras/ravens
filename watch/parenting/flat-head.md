# Flat head — turn the head, keep sleep on the back

## Why this clip

HSE Ireland film, 10:34. The description names Sheila Kiely, Physiotherapy Manager at Sligo University Hospital, on how to notice a flat spot, what to do at home, and when to get help. NHS Tayside physiotherapy links this same film (from 2:29) as the activity demo for head turning and head shape. The written home steps are the HSE plagiocephaly page, reviewed 11 April 2025: https://www2.hse.ie/conditions/plagiocephaly/. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-11-08.

## Cues to steal

- Sleep stays on the back. Change which way the head points each time you put the baby down, including a slight turn once they are asleep. Turn the cot so they look the other way to see you.
- While awake, tummy time every day. If the flat side is the right, set a toy so they spend more time looking left. A rolled towel can prop the elbows under the shoulders for that tummy-time spell.
- Swap the side you use for feeds and for carrying. Cut down time in the car seat and the pram; a sling or carrier is the swap for those awake stretches.
- A mild flat spot usually needs no physiotherapist. The page says it does not press on the brain and is not a developmental problem. Serious cases are the ones referred on for neck stretches.

## Limits / do not apply when

- The rolled towel is the awake tummy-time prop on the HSE list. It is not a pillow, and it does not go in the cot. Back sleep and a clear cot stay [newborn-safer-sleep](./newborn-safer-sleep.md). Awake tummy time stays [tummy-time](./tummy-time.md). Do not import that film's later minute targets onto this note.
- Neck stretches are not a home drill from this clip. HSE says a physiotherapist teaches them only after a serious case is referred. In Finland that door is neuvola. A baby who always looks one way, or whose neck looks tight, is a neuvola question, not a 10-second home stretch.
- The HSE page says special helmets have no evidence behind them. Do not add one from a shop video.
- Car-seat time still uses [infant-car-seat](./infant-car-seat.md) for every car trip. Less time in the seat is about floors and arms at home, not an unclipped baby in a moving car. Carrying stays [baby-sling](./baby-sling.md) and [baby-carry-positions](./baby-carry-positions.md).
- This is HSE wording. Neuvola wins on the day. Do not invent a birth date or change Current stage.
