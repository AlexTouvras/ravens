# Labour — partner support

## Why this clip

Official NHS trust midwives (Mendip Birth Centre, North Bristol) walk a partner through what to do in the room: be present, hydrate, massage, change position, know the birth preferences, ask the midwife. 6:36. Confirmed via YouTube oEmbed 2026-08-23.

## Cues to steal

- The job is company, a hand, a face wipe, and drinks — not coaching from a script.
- Know the birth preferences so you can help them choose in the moment; ask the midwife if you are stuck.
- Massage shoulders/back; help them move; breathe with them if they want that.
- Pack snacks for **you** as well; keep an eye on your own water.

## Limits / do not apply when

- This is **UK** trust logistics, filmed during COVID visiting rules (one partner, PPE, free parking, no showers). Finnish unit visiting, parking, and food rules win.
- Not a diagnosis. Not a substitute for the midwife on call or 112.
- Do not invent a date of birth from this clip.
