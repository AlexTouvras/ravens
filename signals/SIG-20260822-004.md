# Fortum Elmera tender — 88.59% at 18 Sep close; window extended to 2 Oct

## Why open

Fortum Oyj's recommended voluntary cash tender of NOK 47 per share for Elmera Group ASA is still in the shareholder-acceptance window. Fortum's 21 Sep 2026 stock-exchange release reports that as of the initial offer period’s expiry at **16:30 CEST on 18 Sep 2026**, and subject to customary verification, the offeror had acceptances for **101,302,124** Elmera shares — about **88.59%** of issued and outstanding share capital and voting rights (**92.64%** excluding Elmera treasury shares). Of those, 8,074,564 shares (about 7.1% / 7.4% ex-treasury) are pre-acceptances from the 20 Aug offer document. The same print restates that the offer period was extended on 18 Sep to **2 Oct 2026 at 16:30 CEST**; NOK 47 per share and the other offer-document terms are unchanged. The **>90%** minimum-acceptance closing condition is therefore **not yet met** on an issued-and-outstanding basis (it is met only if treasury shares are excluded). Official action on a liquid Nasdaq Helsinki name; not yet a position decision. Distinct from [SIG-20260910-001](./SIG-20260910-001.md) (Loviisa/Google PPA).

## Watch for

- Acceptance-rate disclosures versus the 90% minimum on **issued-and-outstanding** capital (not ex-treasury), and any further extension past 2 Oct 16:30 CEST
- Other remaining closing conditions or a completion/lapse announcement
- Any Ledger position decision tied to Fortum's expanding Nordic energy-retail scale
- Expire if the offer completes or lapses with no further Ledger-relevant catalyst
