# Fortum Elmera tender — offer expired 2 Oct at 91.61%; settlement expected about 23 Oct

## Why open

Fortum Oyj's recommended voluntary cash tender of NOK 47 per share for Elmera Group ASA is **past the acceptance window**. Fortum's 2 Oct 2026 stock-exchange release (Cision, 20:00 EEST / 19:00 CET) says the offer period expired at **16:30 CEST on 2 October 2026**. As of expiry, and subject to customary verification, the offeror had acceptances for **104,755,535** shares — about **91.61%** of issued and outstanding share capital and voting rights (**95.80%** excluding Elmera treasury shares). Regulatory Approvals (Section 3.4 (c)) and Minimum Acceptance (Section 3.4 (a)) remain satisfied as previously announced. This release is the settlement notification under Offer Document section 3.11. Settlement is currently expected **on or about 23 October 2026**, still subject to the other section 3.4 closing conditions remaining satisfied or being waived. After completion, Fortum intends a compulsory acquisition of the remaining shares and steps to delist Elmera from Euronext Oslo Børs. Official action on a liquid Nasdaq Helsinki name; not yet a position decision. Distinct from [SIG-20260910-001](./SIG-20260910-001.md) (Loviisa/Google PPA, expired). Same tender — no second SIG.

## Watch for

- Settlement on or about 23 Oct 2026, a waiver or failure of the remaining section 3.4 conditions, or a lapse — **not** a further acceptance-percentage hunt
- A squeeze-out / Oslo Børs delist timetable after completion
- Any Ledger position decision tied to Fortum's expanding Nordic energy-retail scale
- Expire if settlement completes or the offer lapses with no further Ledger-relevant catalyst
