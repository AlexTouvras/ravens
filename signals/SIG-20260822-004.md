# Fortum Elmera tender — 90.02% accepted; Minimum Acceptance satisfied; window still 2 Oct

## Why open

Fortum Oyj's recommended voluntary cash tender of NOK 47 per share for Elmera Group ASA is still in the shareholder-acceptance window, but the **>90% Minimum Acceptance** closing condition is now **met**. Fortum's 25 Sep 2026 stock-exchange release (Cision, 13:20 EEST) reports that, subject to customary verification, the offeror has acceptances for **102,940,761** Elmera shares — about **90.02%** of issued and outstanding share capital and voting rights (**94.14%** excluding Elmera treasury shares). That satisfies Offer Document Section 3.4 (a). Regulatory Approvals (Section 3.4 (c)) remain satisfied as announced 3 Sep. Completion is still subject to the **other closing conditions** remaining satisfied until settlement or being waived. The offer period is unchanged at **2 Oct 2026 16:30 CEST**, with Fortum still reserving the right to extend to a maximum ending **30 Oct 2026**. After completion, Fortum intends a compulsory acquisition of remaining shares and steps to delist Elmera from Euronext Oslo Børs. Official action on a liquid Nasdaq Helsinki name; not yet a position decision. Distinct from [SIG-20260910-001](./SIG-20260910-001.md) (Loviisa/Google PPA).

## Watch for

- Remaining closing conditions, settlement/completion, or a lapse — **not** a further 90% hunt
- Any extension past 2 Oct 16:30 CEST (max 30 Oct) or a squeeze-out / Oslo Børs delist timetable after completion
- Any Ledger position decision tied to Fortum's expanding Nordic energy-retail scale
- Expire if the offer completes or lapses with no further Ledger-relevant catalyst
