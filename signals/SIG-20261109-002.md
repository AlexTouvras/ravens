# Veikkaus Data Scientist and Senior Data Scientist, Helsinki — apply by 30 Nov

## Why open

Veikkaus Group’s own careers page lists **Data Scientist** as full-time and permanent in Helsinki. The role is at headquarters in Pohjois-Haaga, hybrid with three on-site days a week, and says apply by **30 Nov 2026** (“no later than 30.11.2026”). It sits on the Personalization team and builds production recommendation and personalization models, including churn prediction, in a responsible-gaming context. The same careers index lists **Senior Data Scientist** at `/en/careers/open-positions/senior-data-scientist-0`. The senior page adds mentoring other data scientists. Inquiries on both pages go to Miki Sulander, Head of Analytics. The expired Head of Analytics listing ([SIG-20261026-002](./SIG-20261026-002.md)) is a different ad and stays expired.
