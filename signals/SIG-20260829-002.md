# ECB 2.50% deposit from 16 Sep; FOMC hikes 25bp to 3.75–4.00% from 17 Sep

## Why open

The ECB Governing Council’s **10 Sep 2026** monetary-policy decision raises the three key ECB interest rates by **25 basis points**: deposit facility **2.50%**, main refinancing operations **2.65%**, and marginal lending facility **2.90%**, effective **16 September 2026**. The FOMC’s **16 Sep 2026** statement (official Board HTML, released 2:00 p.m. EDT) then raises the federal-funds target range by **1/4 percentage point to 3-3/4 to 4 percent**, effective **17 Sep 2026**, by a **12–0** vote, and continues ample reserves. The Fed statement says activity is expanding at a solid pace, uncertainty remains elevated in part from geopolitics, job gains have kept pace with the workforce, unemployment has changed little, and inflation remains elevated; the action is to support a timelier return to the 2 percent goal.
