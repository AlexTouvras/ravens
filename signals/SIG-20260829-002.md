# ECB raises three key rates 25bp to 2.50 / 2.65 / 2.90 from 16 Sep

## Why open

The ECB Governing Council’s **10 Sep 2026** monetary-policy decision (official press release) raises the three key ECB interest rates by **25 basis points**: deposit facility **2.50%**, main refinancing operations **2.65%**, and marginal lending facility **2.90%**, effective **16 September 2026**. Staff baseline headline inflation averages **3.0% in 2026, 2.5% in 2027 and 2.1% in 2028**; inflation excluding energy and food **2.5% / 2.6% / 2.3%**; growth **0.9% / 1.4% / 1.5%** (2026–27 growth revised up vs June; 2026 inflation unchanged, 2027–28 inflation revised up). The Council remains data-dependent and meeting-by-meeting and is **not pre-committing** to a particular rate path. APP/PEPP portfolios keep declining with no reinvestment. This is the printed official action that the 29 Aug preview ([FIND-20260829-002](../inbox/2026-08-29.md)) was watching. Macro/regime-shift dual-stream is satisfied via the ECB primary plus an independent Guardian wrap (10 Sep, “ECB raises interest rates to 2.5%”). Relevant to Ledger’s discount-rate / Nordic-equity overlay for the rest of September. Not a durable construction rule.

## Watch for

- FOMC **15–16 Sep 2026** — the next dated US-policy overlay, not another ECB preview
- Any ECB signalling on further hikes beyond the 16 Sep effective date
- A Ledger duration or EUR-exposure decision tied to the 2.50% deposit floor
