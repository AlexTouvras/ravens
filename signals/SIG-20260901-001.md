# UPM EGM approves the partial demerger that will list WISA Group

## Why open

UPM-Kymmene Corporation's 31 Aug 2026 stock-exchange release (15:30 EEST) says the Extraordinary General Meeting held that day **resolved on the partial demerger** of UPM in accordance with the 29 Apr 2026 demerger plan, approved the plan, and, conditional on completion of the demerger, resolved to incorporate an independent company named **WISA Group Plc**, approved its Articles of Association, elected a six-member WISA board chaired by **Tapio Korpeinen**, and authorised WISA's board to issue up to **25,000,000** shares and to acquire or take as pledge up to **50,000,000** of WISA's own shares. EGM minutes are due on upm.com/egm2026 by **14 Sep 2026**.
