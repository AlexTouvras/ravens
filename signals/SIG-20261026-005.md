# Nokia keeps the 2026 profit range and lowers free-cash-flow conversion to 35–55%

## Why open

Nokia Corporation’s interim report of 22 Oct 2026 (08:00 EEST) says the full-year financial outlook is unchanged: comparable operating profit of **EUR 2.1 billion to EUR 2.6 billion**. In the assumptions under that outlook, Nokia marks free-cash-flow conversion from comparable operating profit as an update to **35% to 55%**, and says it is lowering that assumption because of working-capital investment in 2026 to secure supply for 2027 plus accelerated restructuring, including an additional **EUR 400 million** of working capital this year. Restructuring and associated charges are marked as an update at **EUR 1 100 million**, and related cash outflows as an update at **EUR 800–900 million**. Q3 comparable operating profit was **EUR 605 million**. The same release says the board resolved a dividend of **EUR 0.03** per share, record date **27 Oct 2026**, payment **5 Nov 2026**. The next scheduled results are Q4 and full-year 2026 on **28 Jan 2027**. This is the company’s own updated base case inside an unchanged profit range. It is an official-action signal through the dividend payment, not a stock-setup and not a position. The expired Euro Stoxx 50 inclusion watch ([SIG-20260903-001](./SIG-20260903-001.md)) stays expired.

## Watch for

- The 27 Oct 2026 dividend record date and the 5 Nov 2026 payment of EUR 0.03
- A further inside-information release that changes the 35–55% free-cash-flow conversion band before this signal expires
- Q4 and full-year results on 28 Jan 2027 are the next scheduled figures print — a later finding, not a reason to hold this signal open until January
- Expire after the 5 Nov payment if the conversion band is unchanged
- UPM’s WISA listing stays on [SIG-20261026-004](./SIG-20261026-004.md)
