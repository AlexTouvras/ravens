# Enersense cuts 2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance to EUR 15–18 million

## Why open

Enersense International Plc’s inside-information release of 18 Nov 2026 at 21:00 EET lowers 2026 guidance because of weaker than expected performance in Estonia and in the Connectivity business unit, and because some project revenue may transfer to next year. The new guidance estimates 2026 adjusted EBITDA at **EUR 15–18 million**. The comparison in the release is adjusted EBITDA for the core businesses of **EUR 18.8 million** in 2025. The previous guidance, published 12 Feb 2026, estimated 2026 adjusted EBITDA at **EUR 19–23 million**. The release says that in 2026 Enersense discontinues separate reporting of core-business figures. The next financial report named on the page is the 2026 financial statements bulletin on **17 Feb 2027**. The silent period before that publication begins on 18 Jan 2027. That print is a later finding, not a reason to hold this signal open until February. ESENSE is a Nasdaq Helsinki name. This is one official-action print of a lower base case. It is a guidance cut, not a fear-dip and not a position. Oriola stays on [SIG-20261116-003](./SIG-20261116-003.md). Tokmanni stays on [SIG-20261109-004](./SIG-20261109-004.md). Kempower stays on [SIG-20261109-005](./SIG-20261109-005.md).
