# Boliden agrees share-and-cash deal for majority stake in Nexa Resources, tender offer to follow

## Why open

Boliden AB (Nasdaq Stockholm Large Cap) announced on 27 Aug 2026 a definitive agreement to acquire Votorantim's 64.68% stake in Nexa Resources S.A. (NYSE: NEXA) for newly issued Boliden shares (0.250x per Nexa share, implying USD 15.29/share and a total equity value of ~USD 2,025m), expected to close Q1 2027 subject to a Boliden EGM and regulatory approval. Boliden has committed to launch a cash voluntary tender offer for Nexa's remaining minority shares after closing, and the deal is expected to be immediately accretive to Boliden's EPS (contributing >8%). Boliden is a highly liquid Nordnet-tradable Nasdaq Stockholm name — an official-action catalyst on a single primary source (Boliden's own stock exchange release), sufficient without a second corroborating stream. Ledger long-only book context, not yet a position decision.

## Watch for

- Boliden EGM outcome and regulatory approvals ahead of the expected Q1 2027 close
- The share-count dilution (+7.0%) against the claimed >8% EPS accretion once realized
- Launch terms and price of the follow-on cash tender offer for Nexa's remaining minority shares
- Any Ledger long-only entry/add decision tied to this catalyst
