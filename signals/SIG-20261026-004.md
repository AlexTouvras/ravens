# UPM applied to list WISA on Nasdaq Helsinki; trading expected about 2 Nov

## Why open

UPM-Kymmene Corporation’s stock-exchange release of 22 Oct 2026 at 22:30 EEST says UPM has filed a listing application with Nasdaq Helsinki for **WISA Group Plc** shares on the regulated market. Demerger completion is expected to be registered in the Finnish Trade Register on or about **31 Oct 2026**. Trading is expected to start on or about **2 Nov 2026** under the code **WISA**, ISIN **FI4000603345**. Shareholders are to receive one new WISA share for each UPM share. UPM’s own shares take a new ISIN, **FI4000603337**, and keep the trading code **UPM**. The Nasdaq view of the same release (published 22 Oct 2026 21:30 CEST) carries that text; it is the exchange distribution, not a second analysis. This is the listing-application step after the August EGM approval. That earlier watch ([SIG-20260901-001](./SIG-20260901-001.md)) expired 15 Sep 2026 and stays expired. Official action on a liquid Nasdaq Helsinki name. It is a catalyst watch through the expected first trade, not a position.

## Watch for

- Trade Register registration on or about 31 Oct 2026, and WISA trading on or about 2 Nov 2026 under code WISA
- A later UPM release that moves either date, withdraws the application, or changes the one-for-one share ratio
- UPM’s trading code staying UPM while the ISIN becomes FI4000603337
- Expire once trading has started with no remaining dated catalyst, or if the demerger is withdrawn
- Nokia’s 2026 free-cash-flow assumption stays on [SIG-20261026-005](./SIG-20261026-005.md); Digitalist’s 30 Oct review stays on [SIG-20261019-005](./SIG-20261019-005.md)
