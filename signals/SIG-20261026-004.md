# UPM demerger is registered; WISA starts trading on Nasdaq Helsinki on 2 Nov

## Why open

UPM-Kymmene Corporation’s stock-exchange release of 31 Oct 2026 at 9:00 EET says the partial demerger was registered with the Finnish Trade Register that day. Nasdaq Helsinki has approved the listing application. Trading in WISA Group’s shares commences on **2 Nov 2026** under the code **WISA**, ISIN **FI4000603345**. UPM’s shares take the new ISIN **FI4000603337** and keep the trading code **UPM**. Shareholders receive one new WISA share for each UPM share, registered in book-entry accounts on or about 2 Nov 2026. No consideration is issued for UPM treasury shares. The number of UPM shares is unchanged. This is the registration and the approved listing, after the 22 Oct listing application on this same signal. Nasdaq’s own notice of 2 Nov 2026 at 08:30 CET says trading in WISA commences that day on the Nasdaq Helsinki Main Market (Mid Cap, Industrials) after the partial demerger. That notice does not restate the ISINs or the one-for-one ratio; those stay on the UPM release. The August EGM watch ([SIG-20260901-001](./SIG-20260901-001.md)) expired 15 Sep 2026 and stays expired. Official action on a liquid Nasdaq Helsinki name. The first-trade date is today, so this stays watching only through today’s expiry. It is not a position and not a months-to-years setup.
