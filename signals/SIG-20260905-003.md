# AAP 1-month visit topics — 8–12 feeds/day, ~½ lb weekly gain (US figures)

## Why open

AAP's 1- and 2-month health-supervision page (updated 13 Feb 2026) is useful **lookahead** for week 4 / the 1-month mark (20 Sep): breastfed infants still need **8–12 feeds/day**, exclusive milk without water or juice. The **½ lb/week** gain figure disagrees with THL's 150–200 g/week — do not promote the US ounces as the Finnish household rule.

## Watch for

- LUVN 4–6 week visit completed, or Child born + 4 weeks passed (20 Sep) → expire
- A Finnish primary source that restates 8–12 feeds at 1 month → consider promoting that claim into [coming-week](../knowledge/parenting/coming-week.md) and drop the US weight line
- THL/Terveyskirjasto change to weekly gain → update [thl-0-1-month-development-growth](../knowledge/parenting/thl-0-1-month-development-growth.md)
