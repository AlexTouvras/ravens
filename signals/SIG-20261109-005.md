# Kempower cuts 2026 revenue growth to about 5% and expects operative EBIT below 2025

## Why open

Kempower’s inside-information release of 3 Nov 2026 at 19:35 EET lowers 2026 revenue growth to **approximately 5%** versus 2025, from 10–25%, and says 2026 operative EBIT is expected to be **lower than 2025**, when operative EBIT was EUR -12.4 million. The previous outlook, specified on 23 Jul 2026, expected operative EBIT to improve significantly. The release cites sharply weaker demand, delayed investment decisions, and possible fourth-quarter provisions and write-downs related to the fixed-cost program. Preliminary third-quarter figures in that release are order intake EUR 60.8 million, revenue EUR 66.2 million, and operative EBIT EUR -1.7 million. The 4 Nov interim report restates the same full-year outlook and raises the fixed-cost savings target to more than EUR 15 million a year, with full run-rate from the third quarter of 2027, replacing the more-than-EUR 5 million target from 23 Jul. That report is the company’s own figures print, not a second analysis. KEMPOWER is a Nasdaq Helsinki name. This is one official-action print of the company’s updated base case. It is a catalyst watch through the next figures print that moves either line. The 2027 savings run-rate is context on the same report, not a reason to hold this signal open until 2027. It is a guidance cut, not a fear-dip and not a position. Tokmanni’s cut stays on [SIG-20261109-004](./SIG-20261109-004.md).

## Watch for

- A later Kempower release that moves the approximately 5% revenue-growth line or the “operative EBIT lower than 2025” line — log it here
- The 4 Nov interim report already restated this outlook; do not open a second SIG for that restatement or for the EUR 15 million savings target
- Do not treat the lower growth line as a fear-dip; the company published it as the new full-year base case
- Expire on 23 Nov 2026 if no further official-action print has arrived
