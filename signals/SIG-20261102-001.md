# Tieto cuts 2026 organic growth to -6% to -5% and raises the adjusted EBITA margin

## Why open

Tieto Corporation’s inside-information release of 26 Oct 2026 at 19:15 EET lowers full-year 2026 organic growth to **-6% to -5%**, from -5% to -3%, citing continued soft demand, the Tieto Tech Consulting transformation, and lower volumes in Tieto Banktech and Tieto Indtech. The same release raises the full-year adjusted operating margin (adjusted EBITA) to **15.5–16.0%**, from 14.8–15.8%. Preliminary third-quarter figures in that release are revenue EUR 389.6 million, organic growth -11%, and adjusted operating margin 18.0%. The 27 Oct interim report restates the same full-year ranges. That report is the company’s own figures print, not a second analysis. TIETO is a Nasdaq Helsinki and Stockholm name. This is one official-action print of the company’s updated base case. It is a catalyst watch through the next figures print that moves either range, not a fear-dip and not a position. Nokia’s profit range stays on [SIG-20261026-005](./SIG-20261026-005.md). WISA’s first trade stays on [SIG-20261026-004](./SIG-20261026-004.md).

## Watch for

- A later Tieto release that moves the -6% to -5% organic-growth range or the 15.5–16.0% adjusted EBITA range — log it here
- Do not treat the lower growth range as a fear-dip; the company published it as the new full-year base case
- Expire on 16 Nov 2026 if no further official-action print has arrived
- Do not fold this into the Nokia guide or the WISA listing
