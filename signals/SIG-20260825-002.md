# SP Group raises FY2026 guidance and runs a DKK 40m buyback after UK bolt-on

## Why open

Danish plastics group SP Group A/S (Nasdaq Copenhagen: SPG) completed, on 19 Aug 2026, the GBP 18.0m cash-and-debt-free acquisition of UK injection moulder OGM Moulding Ltd. (plus up to GBP 6.0m earn-out tied to 2027–28 earnings). Per its 24 Aug 2026 interim report it raised full-year 2026 revenue-growth guidance from 22–28% to 24–30% (margins unchanged) and is running a DKK 40m share buyback through 31 Dec 2026. An official-action catalyst — guidance upgrade plus a bolt-on acquisition plus an active capital-return programme in the same release — corroborated by a second, independent trade-press stream (etmm-online.com), not the same wire rewrite. Ledger long-only book context, not yet a position decision.

## Watch for

- Guidance delivery against the raised 24–30% revenue-growth range at Q3/FY2026 reporting
- Buyback pace against the DKK 40m cap through year-end
- Earn-out resolution tied to OGM Moulding's 2027–28 earnings
- Any Ledger long-only entry decision tied to this catalyst
