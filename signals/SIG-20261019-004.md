# LUVN — close contacts of someone especially susceptible to severe influenza book December

## Why open

LUVN’s notice of 12 Oct 2026 says influenza-vaccine deliveries are delayed, so some bookable appointments move later, and that some influenza booking has been closed for now while other times remain. Already booked times do not need to be cancelled or moved. If the free vaccine is because the person belongs to the close circle of someone especially susceptible to severe influenza, LUVN says to book for December. The delay applies only to the injectable vaccine, so it does not affect the nasal spray given to 2–6-year-olds. LUVN expects supplies in Finland before the epidemic is forecast to intensify. Age window for this household read: infant `age-2-6m` (the finding’s bucket). The page’s instruction is an adult close-contact booking for this season, through December 2026. Named consumer: household. Time-bounded delivery delay — signal until the December window closes. Current stage on the parenting hub stays as the human set it.

## Watch for

- A close-contact adult whose free influenza shot is for someone especially susceptible: book a December appointment; leave an existing booking in place
- The injectable delay is this season’s supply slip; the 2–6-year-old nasal spray is outside that slip
- A later LUVN notice that reopens earlier booking for close contacts, or that says December slots are full
- Expire after December 2026, or sooner if LUVN withdraws the December instruction
- Keep the oral rotavirus 2/3/5-month note and the injectable five-in-one 3/5/12-month note as they are
