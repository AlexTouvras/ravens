# LUVN — more influenza vaccine has arrived; close-contact December booking is not restated

## Why open

LUVN’s notice of 9 Nov 2026 at 6:00 says the wellbeing services county has received more influenza vaccines after the earlier delivery delays, bookable times are now available, and everyone entitled to a free vaccination should book as soon as possible. The free influenza examples on that page are everyone aged 65 and over, pregnant people, children under 7, people living in care homes, and people in a medical risk group for severe influenza. Vaccinations follow THL recommendations. The 9 Nov notice does not repeat the 12 Oct instruction, and it does not withdraw it: if the free vaccine is because the person belongs to the close circle of someone especially susceptible to severe influenza, the 12 Oct notice still says to book for December. The 9 Nov notice also does not repeat the injectable-only delay or the line that the nasal spray for 2–6-year-olds was unaffected. Those 12 Oct lines stay until LUVN withdraws them. Age window for this household read: infant `age-2-6m` (the finding’s bucket). The under-7 example is the free-group line on the 9 Nov page, not a new infant dosing schedule. Named consumer: household. Time-bounded supply and booking window through December 2026 — signal only. Current stage on the parenting hub stays as the human set it.

## Watch for

- Someone in a free group named on the 9 Nov page (65 and over, pregnant, under 7, care home, medical risk group): book as soon as possible; times are available again
- A close-contact adult whose free influenza shot is for someone especially susceptible: the last explicit LUVN instruction is still the 12 Oct December booking, because the 9 Nov notice does not withdraw it
- Leave an existing booking in place unless a later notice says to move it
- Expire after December 2026, or sooner if LUVN withdraws the December instruction or says the new doses have run out
- Keep the oral rotavirus 2/3/5-month note and the injectable five-in-one 3/5/12-month note as they are
- Do not read the under-7 example as an infant dose schedule
