# Dollar General raises FY2026 guidance and plans to resume share buybacks

## Why open

Dollar General Corporation (NYSE: DG) reported Q2 FY2026 results (27 Aug 2026 SEC 8-K exhibit) — net sales up 5.2% to $11.3bn, same-store sales up 3.5%, diluted EPS up 33.3% to $2.48 — and raised full-year FY2026 guidance to net sales growth of 4.0%–4.3% (from 3.7%–4.2%), same-store sales growth of 2.5%–2.9% (from 2.2%–2.7%), and diluted EPS of $7.80–$8.00 (from $7.20–$7.45). Management also said it plans to resume share repurchases in Q3 FY2026, targeting up to $700m in the second half, funded from cash on hand — a distinct capital-return catalyst on top of the guidance beat. Already-reported official action on a single primary SEC filing, which the finance filters treat as sufficient without a second corroborating stream. A liquid US mega-cap catalyst for Ledger's long-only book; not yet a position decision.

## Watch for

- Guidance delivery against the raised ranges (net sales, same-store sales, diluted EPS) at the next quarterly report
- Actual buyback activity starting in Q3 FY2026 against the up-to-$700m H2 target
- Any Ledger long-only entry decision tied to this catalyst
