# G City subsequent Citycon tender period preliminarily lifts holding to 91.05%

## Why open

Citycon Oyj's 28 Aug 2026 stock-exchange release (Cision) reports G City Ltd.'s preliminary subsequent-offer-period result: 2,180,672 shares validly tendered in the 6–27 Aug 2026 subsequent period (about 1.2% of Citycon shares and votes), taking G City plus fully owned Gazit Europe Netherlands to 167,133,085 shares, or about **91.05%** of all shares and voting rights. That prints above Finland's **90% compulsory-redemption** line. The Offeror said it would confirm the final subsequent-period result on or about **31 Aug 2026** and reserved the right to keep buying Citycon shares on Nasdaq Helsinki or otherwise as allowed. Official-action catalyst on a liquid Nasdaq Helsinki name — next prints (final result, squeeze-out / delisting notice) are the concrete Ledger watch items.

## Watch for

- Further on-market Nasdaq Helsinki purchases that change the minority-position math
- Expire if the squeeze-out completes with no remaining Nordnet-tradable catalyst, or if the final result falls back under 90% with no further official action
