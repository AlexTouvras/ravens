# Power BI standalone and app-scoped Copilot end in January 2027; semantic views publish a read-only model

## Why open

Microsoft Learn’s current Power BI “what’s new” page is the November 2026 update. It is a living vendor page and does not show a separate article timestamp. Support for standalone Power BI Copilot on the web, in Teams, and in Power BI mobile is planned to end by the end of January 2027. Copilot within reports remains supported. App-scoped Copilot in workspace apps, and its app setting, are scheduled for removal in January 2027. App content, audiences, and permissions are unchanged, and Copilot within eligible reports remains available. Both retirement rows are marked currently in preview on that page. The same page lists semantic views in preview: publishing a semantic view creates a managed, read-only Direct Lake on OneLake semantic model, and the view remains the source of truth. Named consumer: Power BI portfolio / Orbit Analytics. This is a scheduled product change plus a preview modeling path, not a durable prefer/avoid rule. It does not change the read-only model-setting note ([copilot-readonly-mcp-default-on](../knowledge/data-bi/copilot-readonly-mcp-default-on.md)) and it does not change the Direct Lake prefer-rule ([direct-lake-onelake-preferred](../knowledge/data-bi/direct-lake-onelake-preferred.md)).

## Watch for

- A report or app workflow that depends on standalone Copilot (web, Teams, or mobile) or on app-scoped Copilot — move that path to Copilot inside an eligible report before the end of January 2027
- App content, audiences, and permissions stay as they are when the app-scoped Copilot setting is removed
- A semantic view leaving preview, or a publish that no longer creates a read-only Direct Lake model — log it here before promoting a modeling rule
- Expire on 31 Jan 2027 if the retirement has landed and semantic views are still only a preview publish path
- Do not rewrite the read-only Copilot/MCP model setting, and do not treat a semantic view as a reason to drop Direct Lake on OneLake
