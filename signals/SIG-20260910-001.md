# Fortum 22-year Loviisa nuclear PPA with Google — up to 50% capacity, ~1.4pp RONA

## Why open

Fortum Corporation’s 9 Sep 2026 inside-information stock-exchange release (10:00 EEST) says Fortum and Google signed a 22-year life-extension power-purchase agreement covering up to 50% of Loviisa nuclear plant capacity. Deliveries start in 2028 at a smaller volume and reach 50% in 2030–2049. Fortum says the PPA supplies the revenue certainty to continue the ~EUR 1 billion lifetime-extension programme through 2050 (about 80% of projects / EUR 700 million capex still pending investment decisions), is expected to enable a further 10 MW uprate on top of the already-planned 38 MW uprate due live in 2028, and is expected to raise Group comparable RONA by about 1.4 percentage points over time once 50% of generation is contracted (toward the 14% long-term RONA target; 11.0% LTM at end-Q2 2026). The PPA will sit in Fortum’s long-term hedge ratio. The same release records an MoU on new nuclear, renewables, flexibility, and a 94 MW Kajaani battery Fortum will optimize for Google. Official action on a liquid Nasdaq Helsinki utility; Google’s 9 Sep clean-energy Finland blog is an independent second stream. Not the Elmera tender (still open to 18 Sep). Not yet a Ledger position decision.

## Watch for

- Any follow-on IR print that sizes remaining Loviisa lifetime-extension investment decisions or restates the ~1.4pp RONA lift
- A completion, amendment, or unwind of the Google PPA / MoU / Kajaani battery optimization
- Any Ledger long-only entry or size decision tied to Fortum’s contracted nuclear offtake
- Expire if no further official-action print this window; do not fold into SIG-20260822-004 (Elmera acceptance is a different catalyst)
