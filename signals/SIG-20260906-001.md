# AAP supervised tummy time — build toward 15–30 minutes/day by 7 weeks

## Why open

AAP’s HealthyChildren safe-sleep explainer (last updated 8 Jan 2026) is useful **lookahead** for the coming weeks: an awake adult should supervise awake tummy time every day, starting short after hospital discharge and building to **at least 15–30 minutes/day by 7 weeks** (about 8 Oct from Child born 20 Aug). This is play while awake, not a sleep position. Finnish how-to this week stays **short, pleasant sessions** — [fi-infant-tummy-time](../knowledge/parenting/fi-infant-tummy-time.md) — not a US minute quota tonight.

## Watch for

- Child born + 7 weeks (8 Oct) or a comfortable 15–30 minute daily total → expire
- Terveyskirjasto/THL adding a Finnish minute target → consider promoting that figure into the tummy-time note and drop the US volume line
- Flat-head / motor concern raised at LUVN 4–6 week visit → keep the Finnish quality-over-duration rule; do not invent a US well-child booking
