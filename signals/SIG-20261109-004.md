# Tokmanni cuts 2026 revenue to EUR 1,780–1,830 million and comparable EBIT to EUR 75–90 million

## Why open

Tokmanni Group’s inside-information release of 5 Nov 2026 at 14:00 lowers 2026 revenue guidance to **EUR 1,780–1,830 million**, from EUR 1,780–1,860 million, and comparable EBIT to **EUR 75–90 million**, from EUR 85–105 million. The previous ranges were the 6 Mar 2026 guidance. The release cites careful purchasing and a smaller average basket in Finland in the third quarter, and weaker consumer confidence in Finland and Sweden in October. Preliminary January–September figures in that release are revenue EUR 1,266.5 million and comparable EBIT EUR 33.1 million. The 6 Nov business review restates the same full-year ranges and says the guidance was issued on 5 Nov. That review is the company’s own figures print, not a second analysis. TOKMAN is a Nasdaq Helsinki name. This is one official-action print of the company’s updated base case. It is a catalyst watch through the next figures print that moves either range. It is a guidance cut, not a fear-dip and not a position. Tieto’s outlook stays on [SIG-20261102-001](./SIG-20261102-001.md). The rate holds stay on [SIG-20261102-002](./SIG-20261102-002.md).
