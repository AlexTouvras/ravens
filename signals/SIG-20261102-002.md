# ECB holds at 2.50% / 2.65% / 2.90%; Fed holds at 3.75–4%

## Why open

The ECB Governing Council’s monetary-policy decision of 29 Oct 2026 keeps the deposit facility, the main refinancing operations, and the marginal lending facility unchanged at **2.50%**, **2.65%**, and **2.90%**. The decision says energy prices have continued to rise, with upside risks to the inflation outlook and downside risks to the growth outlook. Underlying inflation has remained broadly stable so far and wages do not show a material response at this stage. Financing conditions have tightened since the previous meeting. The Council stays data-dependent and meeting-by-meeting and is not pre-committing to a rate path. APP and PEPP portfolios keep declining because maturing principal is not reinvested. Suomen Pankki’s same-day release states the same hold and the same three rates. The Federal Reserve’s FOMC statement of 28 Oct 2026, released at 2:00 p.m. EDT, maintains the target range for the federal funds rate at **3-3/4 to 4 percent** by a 10–2 vote and continues maintaining ample reserves. Lisa D. Cook and Beth M. Hammack voted against the action and preferred to raise the target range by 1/4 percentage point at this meeting. The FOMC print is logged here so the two holds stay on one rates signal. This does not reopen expired [SIG-20260829-002](./SIG-20260829-002.md). These are printed decisions, not a position.

## Watch for

- The next Governing Council or FOMC decision that changes either range — log it on this signal; do not open a second rates SIG
- A later Council text that drops the line that financing conditions have tightened since the previous meeting
- Expire on 16 Nov 2026 if neither committee has printed a new decision
