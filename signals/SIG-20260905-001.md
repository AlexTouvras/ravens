# FCCA clears Terveystalo’s Hohde dental deal; clinic divestments still due around October

## Why open

Terveystalo Plc's 3 Sep 2026 stock-exchange release (12:25 EEST; Nasdaq Helsinki reprint on Inderes — terveystalo.com IR/newsroom 404'd on the Huginn run) says the Finnish Competition and Consumer Authority (KKV/FCCA) approved Terveystalo Healthcare Oy's acquisition of Hohde Group, announced 23 Dec 2025, subject to commitments: Terveystalo will sell its private dental-care operations in Savonlinna, Mikkeli, and Seinäjoki to Emal Hammaslääkärit Oy under a June 2026 sale agreement expected to complete around October 2026. Terveystalo estimated the Hohde acquisition itself would complete on 4 Sep 2026; this run does **not** treat that estimate as a confirmed close (no publisher completion URL). Official-action regulatory clearance on a liquid Nasdaq Helsinki healthcare name — Ledger long-only book, not yet a position decision. Single primary (Terveystalo OMX release) is sufficient; KKV's 3 Sep decision page restates the same conditional clearance and the three-city Emal divestment.

## Watch for

- A publisher-page completion print that Hohde actually closed (do not promote aggregator copies)
- October 2026 close of the Savonlinna / Mikkeli / Seinäjoki clinic sales to Emal Hammaslääkärit
- Any further FCCA/KKV or Terveystalo IR revision of the commitments
- Any Ledger long-only entry/add decision tied to this catalyst
