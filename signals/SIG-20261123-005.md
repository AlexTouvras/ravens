# Pydantic AI Harness 0.70.0 fixes two event-loop stalls in secret redaction and ANSI stripping

## Why open

GitHub advisory GHSA-5m5q-5qj4-gx9h, published 2026-11-18T00:04:45Z, rates as high a quadratic-time `jwt` pattern in Pydantic AI Harness secret redaction. Affected versions are `pydantic-ai-harness` 0.16.0 through 0.69.0. The pattern is included by default in `redact_secrets` and `secret_data()`. Crafted text of a few hundred kilobytes can occupy the event loop for tens of seconds to minutes. The patched version is 0.70.0. Until then, the advisory says to leave `jwt` out by naming the patterns you need with `secret_data(only=[...])`. The same 0.70.0 line fixes GHSA-m4cr-m4qc-9hh3, also high and published 2026-11-18T00:04:44Z: with `strip_ansi=True` (named `OverflowingToolOutput` before 0.13.0), ANSI stripping could take quadratic time. That advisory’s affected range is 0.4.0 through 0.69.0, and it says to leave `strip_ansi` at its default `False` until the upgrade. On Pydantic AI v1 the harness cannot be upgraded past 0.4.0; the advisory says to move to v2 and harness 0.70.0, or leave `strip_ansi` off. These are the harness fixes shipped with pydantic-ai v2.70.0. Named consumer: field-card. They do not replace the schema-expansion watch ([SIG-20261109-003](./SIG-20261109-003.md)). They do not change the in-run approval watch ([SIG-20261116-001](./SIG-20261116-001.md)). Default path: signal — upgrade this week. It does not change the github-mcp pin.

## Watch for

- A harness on 0.16.0 through 0.69.0 that redacts secrets with the default `jwt` pattern — move to 0.70.0, or name the patterns you need and leave `jwt` out until then
- A harness on 0.4.0 through 0.69.0 with `strip_ansi=True` — move to 0.70.0, or leave `strip_ansi` at `False`
- A v1 install that cannot take harness 0.70.0 — leave `strip_ansi` off, or move to Pydantic AI v2 and harness 0.70.0
- Promote a prefer/avoid floor only if field-card adopts 0.70.0 and both limits still hold; else expire
- Do not fold this into SIG-20261109-003 or SIG-20261116-001
