# Warsh Jackson Hole — inflation above 2% PCE, financial conditions not restrictive

## Why open

Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's 28 Aug 2026 Jackson Hole keynote ("In Our Time") is now a delivered official primary, not last week's calendar-flag preview (FIND-20260823-004, dropped for a missing second stream). The Fed's own speech page says the 12-month PCE price index stands at 3.7% (six-month 4.1%), that "inflation is running above our 2 percent target" so the Fed's "predominant focus right now should be on prices," that he would be "hard pressed to describe broad financial conditions as restrictive," and that "we must be confident that underlying inflation is moving to our objective, clearly and at sufficient speed. Otherwise, we have work to do." He declined forward guidance or an explicit reaction function ("committed to a discipline, not to a decision"). CNBC's independent market read (not a wire rewrite of the Fed page) reports the 2-year yield +8bp and CME FedWatch September-hike odds at 55.7%. Dual-stream satisfied. A US rates/USD regime read for Ledger ahead of the September FOMC — time-bound, not a durable construction rule.

## Watch for

- The September FOMC decision versus the 55.7% hike odds priced after the speech
- Any follow-on Fed communication that restates or walks back the "work to do" / not-restrictive-conditions line
- A Ledger duration or USD-exposure decision tied to the outcome
