# Raisio now expects 2026 net sales flat with 2025 and comparable EBIT flat or slightly lower

## Why open

Raisio’s investor outlook page states that on 6 Nov 2026 the company lowered its 2026 guidance. Net sales are now projected **at the same level as in 2025**, and comparable EBIT **at the 2025 level or slightly lower**. The previous guidance projected both net sales and comparable EBIT to increase versus 2025. The page attributes the change to weaker-than-expected sales late in 2026 and higher-than-anticipated costs of the ongoing ERP project. Nasdaq Helsinki’s copy of the 6 Nov stock-exchange release (Raisio plc, 6 Nov 2026 at 9:30 EET) states the same new and previous guidance, and adds preliminary January–September net sales of EUR 171.3 million and comparable EBIT of EUR 21.5 million. That release schedules the interim report for **10 Nov 2026**. RAISIO is a Nasdaq Helsinki name. This is one official-action print. Signal through that interim report. It is a guidance cut, not a fear-dip and not a position. Tokmanni stays on [SIG-20261109-004](./SIG-20261109-004.md). Kempower stays on [SIG-20261109-005](./SIG-20261109-005.md).

## Watch for

- The 10 Nov 2026 interim report — log whether it restates this flat / slightly-lower guide or moves either line
- A further inside-information release before that report that restores an increase-versus-2025 guide
- Expire after the 10 Nov print if no further dated catalyst is on the same release
- Do not fold this into the Tokmanni or Kempower signals
