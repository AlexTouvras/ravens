# Orion Nasdaq Helsinki Class B buyback — up to 500,000 shares / EUR 45m

## Why open

On 27 Aug 2026 Orion Corporation (Nasdaq Helsinki: ORNBV) announced that its Board decided to commence acquisition of the company's own shares under the 24 Mar 2026 AGM authorisation: up to 500,000 Class B shares, maximum consideration EUR 45,000,000, purchases on Nasdaq Helsinki at market price from unrestricted equity, starting 1 Sep 2026 at the earliest and running through 31 Dec 2026 at the latest, for share-based incentive plans, executed under MAR Article 5 / Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/1052 via an independently deciding third-party broker. At the Board decision Orion held 268,166 own Class B shares of 141,134,278 shares outstanding. Already-reported official action on a single primary stock-exchange release — finance filters treat that as sufficient without a second stream. A liquid Nordnet-tradable Finnish pharma name with a firm start window (this Tuesday) and a published size/cap. Huginn cited the GlobeNewswire wire of the same release; Muninn verified the claim on Orion's own IR page (GlobeNewswire timed out from this environment).
