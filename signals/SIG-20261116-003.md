# Oriola now allows 2026 adjusted EBITDA to be flat with 2025

## Why open

Oriola Corporation’s stock-exchange release of 11 Nov 2026 at 6:15 p.m. EET revises the 2026 outlook. The fourth-quarter result is not expected to be sufficient to reach the full-year guidance then in force. The release cites higher-than-expected operating expenses in October, mainly driven by disturbances in the Swedish supply chain. The new outlook expects 2026 adjusted EBITDA to be **at the same level as or to increase** from 2025 (**EUR 35.1 million**). The previous outlook, published 25 Feb 2026, expected adjusted EBITDA **to increase** from that 2025 figure. The English outlook page restates both lines and says the revision was published as a stock-exchange release on 11 Nov 2026. That page is the company’s own restatement, not a second analysis. The same release schedules the Financial Statements Release for 2026 on **23 Feb 2027**. The silent period preceding that publication begins on 11 Jan 2027. That print is a later finding, not a reason to hold this signal open until February. The release is distributed to Nasdaq Helsinki. This is one official-action print of a wider base case. It is a guidance revision, not a fear-dip and not a position. Tokmanni stays on [SIG-20261109-004](./SIG-20261109-004.md). Kempower stays on [SIG-20261109-005](./SIG-20261109-005.md).

## Watch for

- A later Oriola release that moves the “same level as or increase” line, or restores an increase-only guide — log it here
- The 23 Feb 2027 financial statements release is the next scheduled figures print — a later finding
- Expire on 30 Nov 2026 if no further official-action print has arrived
- Do not treat the wider range as a fear-dip; the company published it as the new full-year base case
- Do not fold this into the Tokmanni or Kempower signals
